One gun. All recoil.

Recoil Foundry is a physics roguelike inside a hostile factory. Turn your gun's kick into movement, combine 100 upgrades, and fight through 20 rooms across five areas. Shoot down. Go up.

Build it your way with branching upgrades, changing room layouts, and a Daily Run with a shared seed and fixed upgrade choices.

Available now in your browser. Play on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and mouse.

A game by Caleb Guyer.
Trailer features in-engine gameplay, the game's original procedural music, and its sound effects.
