The trailer uses two excerpts: 2:43.276–3:02.476, followed by the original closing passage at 4:09.676–4:23.200. Music begins at 0:06 in the video after an original factory-ambience and footsteps opening. The excerpts join at 0:25.20 with a 25 ms crossfade; the second excerpt includes 25 ms before its aligned start for that overlap. The recording's final accents and natural ring-out are retained. Changes: trimming, a short crossfade and entry fade, level adjustment, a small EQ cut, brief ducking beneath effects, delayed entry, and mixing with Recoil Foundry sound effects. No vocals, other music, artificial closing echo, or title stinger were added. The game itself continues using its existing soundtrack.
