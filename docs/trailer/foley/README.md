# Opening footfalls

Five concrete footstep samples from **Impact Sounds 1.0**, by **Kenney**.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip
- License: CC0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original license notice: [Kenney-License.txt](Kenney-License.txt)

The committed WAVs derive from `Audio/footstep_concrete_000.ogg` through `004.ogg`. They were converted to 48 kHz mono PCM16, high-passed at 95 Hz, low-passed at 6 kHz, level-matched, and given short edge fades. [samples.json](samples.json) records preparation details. No pitch shift is applied.

The opening alternates samples with small level variations and quiet 45/82 ms room reflections. The shared `FOOTSTEPS` timeline drives audible onsets and visible heel plants. The floor contact is fixed, and the character settles onto both feet before raising the gun. The old synthesized kick-like thuds, ringing sine tones and long repeat echoes are removed.

These assets are used only by the offline trailer renderer. They are not shipped with the browser game. CC0 does not require attribution; the upload copy includes a courtesy credit.
