# Dead Signal is here

**The shift ended. The orders didn’t.**

Dead Signal is a free content update for Recoil Foundry. An abandoned communications wing has started issuing orders again. After Furnace, take the upper exit into the Transmission Annex—or stay on the Cooling Works route.

- **Four new rooms in your run, six possible combat layouts.** Climb cable wells, cross broadcast floors, and follow the signal deeper inside.
- **Make the factory fight itself.** Five Subversion upgrades let you reboot defeated machines as temporary blue allies. Choose one stronger ally or a short-lived pair that can overload on shutdown.
- **Two new threats.** Shoot a charging junction to interrupt a remote volley. Keep moving when a Caller records your position.
- **A new synth-rock theme and factory records to uncover.** Some things are better discovered in the Annex.

Still one gun. Still twenty rooms. A new route to take on the next run. Your existing progress stays intact.
