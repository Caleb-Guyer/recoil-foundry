# Rare Miniboss Hunts validation — 4.11.0

The full regular suite passed **2,609 checks** before the final platform adjustment. All **70 affected hunt, archive and Practice checks**, including **34 hunt-specific checks**, pass with the adjusted platforms. TypeScript and the Vite production build passed. The existing shared-content chunk-size warning remains. The release workflow reruns the entire regular suite and eight maximal-build groups before deployment.

Scheduling checks cover deterministic, rare, single-hunt Campaign plans from zone two onward, physically clear optional doors, incompatible special rooms and main-exit skips. Preview URLs and checkpoints reject unknown kinds, duplicate parameters, mixed modes and mismatched revisions. Continue restores the same fight, pending service choice or remaining reroll; Campaign Retry retains the seed. Older saves, Daily and other modes retain their previous rules.

Combat checks use real Matter bodies, normal projectile and beam paths, enemy AI, solid props and line-of-sight damage. They cover finite cable, plate and mine supplies; player-only disarming and plate damage; collision and plate impulses; cable severing; mine warnings, fuses and blocked blasts; recovery openings; cleanup on death; pause and hitstop; and correctly aimed, fully warned fallback volleys after supplies run out. Corner-pressure checks verify that all three machines can reach both floor corners with live attacks. A final center-lane check exposed the Bulwark cannon clipping a platform edge; raising both decks twenty pixels keeps its actual projectile origin clear, and the new regression verifies live pressure there.

Nine preset fights (three machines × pistol, shotgun and nailgun) finish with positive health using ordinary movement and firing. Three additional Campaign checks clear a normal parent patrol, reach the physical side door and defeat its hunt: Cableweaver seed `HUNT-RUN-8`, Bulwark seed `HUNT-RUN-3`, Demolisher seed `HUNT-RUN-2`. These checks do not teleport, change health, suppress attacks or grant invulnerability. A separate full Campaign attempt defeated the Bulwark and claimed its repair, then died at the ordinary room-eight boss; it is not counted as a completed Campaign or a human difficulty assessment.

Progress checks require a live, eligible Campaign hunt victory for its cosmetic and Practice unlock. Practice, isolated previews, cleanup and unrelated enemy kills cannot award them. Service choices preserve the parent room and build; repair caps at full health, and a reroll credit can be spent only once. Practice records and challenges distinguish each machine, while Gauntlet retains its existing roster. Logbook images use the native enemy and arena renderer; cosmetic cards use actual weapon and outfit art.

Production browser QA verified the Bulwark's isolated launch, earned three-machine Practice gallery, preset fight and Retry. A validated temporary local backup exercised Continue at a pending Demolisher reward, its actual Fusekeeper picture, View rewards in Logbook, Back, and choosing the reroll before returning to the cleared parent room. Undo restored the original Room 1 profile with zero discoveries and victories. Compiled temporary fixtures displayed all native portraits, arena photographs and cosmetic rewards; a 480-pixel iframe had no horizontal overflow. Fixtures are ignored and excluded from the deployed build.

Reproduce the focused checks with `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/hunts.test.ts`, the regular suite with `npm run test:ci`, and the production bundle with `npm run build`. [Release notes and isolated test links](../releases/4.11.0.md).
