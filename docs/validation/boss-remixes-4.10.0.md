# Boss Remixes validation — 4.10.0

All **2,564 regular regression checks** passed, with no failures or skips. This includes **38 new remix checks** and eighteen ordinary-input fight completions: all six arenas with each of the pistol, shotgun and nailgun. TypeScript and the Vite production build passed. The existing shared-content chunk-size warning remains. CI runs its eight maximal-build groups separately before deployment; those simulations use Workshop, where remixes are disabled.

Generation checks cover 360 rooms across sixty seeds, including the opening room and all major boss stages. First Campaigns retain original arenas, enabled seeds select deterministic variants, all six variants occur, and alternate bosses keep their own fights. Remix boss health matches normal stage values. Continue and Recent-run history preserve the revision; older saves, Daily, Overtime, Workshop and ordinary Practice retain their encounter schedules. Preview/checkpoint validation rejects unknown variants, conflicting boss kinds, duplicate parameters, mixed modes and unsupported revisions.

Arena checks use live Matter bodies to verify that initial boss hulls and hanging cargo do not overlap terrain, valves stay exposed, Loader supports match the authored braces, and Practice and native archive photographs use the same actual layouts. Switchyard's left cargo was moved clear of the shelf hull during inspection.

Combat checks exercise the scheduled Loader and Press fans, complete warning periods, locked aim and exposed recovery. Condenser tests verify Cold Circuit's two single aimed cycles followed by one purge, and Purge Chamber's eight-ray volleys with four wide gaps, a separate follow-up warning and shifted gaps. The renderer and live attacks use the same angle function. Manual pressure activation opens Press or Condenser armor for its finite window without cancelling an attack; the shared opening cooldown prevents valve cycling from holding armor open.

All eighteen preset fight simulations clear with positive health using ordinary movement and firing against live AI, physics, terrain and machinery. They do not teleport, increase health, disable attacks or grant invulnerability. Preview runs produce no checkpoint writes or completion awards. An initial Cold Circuit shotgun simulation failed; reducing its aimed cycles to one volley each created a distinct, reachable recovery rhythm. The final shotgun check passes alongside every other weapon/arena combination. These checks establish reachable fights, rather than human difficulty measurements.

Progress checks require an actual live eligible Campaign encounter and prior Campaign victory to discover a variant. Unseen variants stay hidden, standard victories cannot substitute for variant access, and previews do not award discoveries. Backups include the six-entry discovery profile, older backups migrate an empty profile, malformed entries fail validation, and Restore/Undo preserve Continue. Practice wins, records, build identities, shared codes and challenge access distinguish the variant from its original boss and from its sibling arena. A win from mismatched arena geometry cannot record a variant result.

Production browser QA launched Crossdock through its named Test button and inspected the actual cargo and braces, isolated Restart flow and Pause mechanic text. A validated synthetic local backup exercised the native Practice gallery, all six actual arena photographs, Purge Chamber setup, preset launch and Retry retention. Undo restored the original local Room 1 profile with no remix discoveries. The compiled native menu was also inspected inside a 480 × 800 iframe: cards use one column, text wraps within their bounds and the dialog scrolls. Production application warning/error logs were empty. A temporary development fixture failed to load a named export in the in-app browser; the compiled fixture loaded and rendered correctly. Temporary fixtures are ignored and excluded from the deployed build.

Reproduce the focused checks with `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/boss-remixes.test.ts`, the regular suite with `npm run test:ci`, and the production bundle with `npm run build`. Playable isolated links are in [the release notes](../releases/4.10.0.md).
