# Boss Gauntlet validation — 4.8.0

All **2,499 regular regression checks** passed, with no failures or skips. The final Gauntlet/Practice/commendation/progress run passed **104 focused checks**, followed by a successful TypeScript/Vite production build. The Pages workflow still runs its eight maximal-build groups before deploying. Vite reports the existing shared-content chunk-size warning; the build succeeds.

The 57 new checks cover all ten actual boss arenas, all 32 five-round routes, Campaign and starting-gun access, four exclusive service choices, health carry, repair caps, legal deterministic drafts, timer freezing, transient physics cleanup, whole-attempt restart and termination. Route fixtures resolve real boss defeat events to exercise every transition. Forged wins, cleanup-only defeats, death, previews and unrelated modes cannot produce a completed record. Campaign checkpoints, ordinary Practice victories, weapon milestones and combat commendations remain isolated.

Six additional runs inside those tests use ordinary movement and firing inputs against actual AI, cover, collision and attack systems: both complete opposing routes with each of the pistol, shotgun and nailgun. These runs carry real health and choose legitimate repairs or offered fittings. They do not teleport, heal between arenas outside the chosen repair, disable attacks or grant invincibility. Each reaches five clears with positive health. Gauntlet bosses use 400/550/750/950/1,150 health by round because only four fittings are available; Campaign and ordinary Practice health are unchanged. Automated completions establish reachable routes, rather than measuring human difficulty.

Record checks reject malformed fields, unknown guns/mods, invalid routes, inconsistent repair budgets, duplicates and oversized collections. Matching gun/route identities keep the fastest combat time, with hits and then shots as ties. Records are cloned and migrate from older profiles. Progress export, restore and Undo preserve a pending Campaign checkpoint alongside the new records. Victor remains locked without its completion commendation and uses the existing appearance, reward-card and Logbook renderers.

Production-browser QA used the final bundle and a disposable localhost profile. Before restore, the fresh title omitted Practice. A synthetic Campaign-clear profile containing three records earned by the ordinary-input simulations enabled Practice → Boss Gauntlet, all earned guns and matching route records. The two opening choices displayed actual Loader/Crane artwork. Selecting Crane started its real arena with the Gauntlet round HUD. Pause explained health carry and whole-attempt restart; Restart returned to round one, 100 health and zero time. Quit retained Continue, and Continue opened the original Cargo trial's pending upgrade offer. The Logbook displayed Five Machines Down and the actual Victor outfit. Import and Undo showed the correct record counts and returned the temporary profile to its original empty state. A direct nailgun preview opened the isolated flow without unlocking the gun or mode.

Separate local component fixtures rendered real Gauntlet menus against an isolated preview Game, without a ProgressStore. These deliberately resolved bosses to inspect service and completion states. Repair changed 61 health to 91 and opened the actual Press/Kiln choice. Visual checks at the normal desktop size and inside a 480 × 800 iframe confirmed readable service cards, single-column boss choices, scrolling and the native Victor reward image. The completion fixture deliberately requested the reward card for visual inspection; real playtest completion does not award or display a new reward. Browser warning/error logs were empty. A service-note spacing overlap and the completion card's Logbook link were fixed before the final build. Temporary tabs, viewport overrides and preview servers were cleaned up.

Reproduce focused checks with `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/gauntlet.test.ts tests/practice.test.ts tests/commendations.test.ts tests/progress.test.ts`, the regular suite with `npm run test:ci`, and the production bundle with `npm run build`.
