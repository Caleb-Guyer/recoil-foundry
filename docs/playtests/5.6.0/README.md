# Recoil Foundry — first ten minutes playtest

**Open for feedback. Target: 5–10 first-time players.** The current build is **5.6.0**. A first session can take about ten minutes; stop sooner whenever you want. You do not need to win, record your screen or be good at roguelikes.

## Play first

1. [Open the normal game on itch.io](https://caleb-guyer.itch.io/recoil-foundry) on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and mouse.
2. Play however you normally would. Use the menus or controls help if you want. You can stop at any time.
3. After playing, share your impressions below. There is no need to take notes during the run.

First-time players are the main focus. Returning players are welcome; say that you have played before. Keep any existing save. There is no reason to reset progress for this test.

<details>
<summary>After playing — three short questions</summary>

1. **What was the best moment?** A specific action, upgrade or encounter helps. It is fine if nothing stood out.
2. **What first felt confusing, unfair or frustrating?** What happened, and what did you expect?
3. **Did you want another run, and why?** Say whether you actually restarted after your first death, tried but could not restart, stopped, or did not die before you finished the session.

Please add whether this was your first time, roughly how long you played, your browser, and the room you reached if you remember. If you watched the trailer, read a guide or had someone help, mention that too.

</details>

## Send feedback

- **Simplest:** reply in the comments on the itch.io launch devlog linked from the [game page](https://caleb-guyer.itch.io/recoil-foundry). An itch.io account is required to comment; a GitHub account is not needed for this route.
- **Structured form:** [submit a first-ten-minutes report on GitHub](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/new?template=first_ten_minutes.yml). This route requires a GitHub account.
- For a reproducible problem, **Feedback** after a run or **Pause → Report an issue → Copy details** prepares the version, seed, room and build. Review the text before sharing it with your feedback.

Feedback posted to itch.io or GitHub is public. No names, email addresses, private files or recordings are requested. Participation is optional and there is no deadline.

This is a self-guided feedback round, not a scheduled hosted session. No participants or completed human sessions have been recorded in the repository yet. The [moderator sheet](moderator.md) keeps observations, reports and possible fixes separate.
