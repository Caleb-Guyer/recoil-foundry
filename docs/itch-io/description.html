Your gun is also your way out.

Shoot down. Go up. Recoil Foundry is a physics roguelike about building one absurd gun and surviving a hostile factory. Every shot moves you. Find upgrades that work together, turn recoil into movement, and make it through twenty rooms.

Watch the official gameplay trailer · Free to play in your browser.

  • 141 upgrades. Combine beams, scattershot, saws, ricochets, explosive rounds, movement fittings and support systems. See how a new fitting changes your existing build before choosing it.
  • Six starting weapons to earn. Begin with the Service pistol, then unlock the Recoil shotgun, Burst nailgun, Twinbore, Coil carbine and Pressure repeater through different accomplishments. Each uses the same upgrade system.
  • Twenty rooms across five factory zones. Fight machines and bosses among moving cargo, presses, coolant, conveyors and rooftop platforms. Changing layouts, enemy variants and optional encounters give each run a different shape.
  • Choose your jobs. Branch into recovery, sabotage, defense and evacuation missions after the opening zone. Discover the Transmission Annex, optional machine hunts and alternate boss arenas. Your route choices can change the factory's final defense.
  • A reason to come back. Take on a shared-seed Daily Run, continue into Overtime, raise the Security level, or build a five-fight Classic or Remix Boss Gauntlet.
  • Discover, unlock and experiment. Collect pictured cosmetic rewards, explore a Logbook with actual game artwork, save Workshop builds and rematch discovered bosses in Practice. A compact next-goal card helps you find your next unlock.

After your first Campaign victory and a remix discovery, turn your discovered boss arenas into a five-fight circuit. Carry health and upgrades between fights, choose a fitting or repair at each service stop, and try Overclocked for stronger bosses and warned counterattacks. Earn new cosmetics and share challenges with records for each gun, route and tier.

Controls

A / D or arrows to move · Space to jump · Mouse to aim · Left click to shoot · Right click for an equipped secondary · Esc / P to pause.

Shooting downward while airborne lifts you up. Start with Controls → Try controls if you want a safe place to learn. Keys, music, sound, and reduced effects are adjustable in Settings.

Progress & help

Designed for Windows desktop/laptop with keyboard and mouse. Browser support and known limitations.

Progress is saved in your browser. Use Settings → Progress to export a backup or restore one. Moving from the GitHub version? Export there, then import here; saves do not transfer between sites automatically.

Found a bug? Settings → Report an issue prepares the details for you to review. Or report it here. In the comments, tell me which build you would play again.
