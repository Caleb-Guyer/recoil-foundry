Dead Signal is here.

The shift ended. The orders didn’t.

An abandoned communications wing is transmitting again. Take the upper exit after Furnace into the Transmission Annex: a new four-room route with six combat layouts, new machines, an original synth-rock theme, and factory records to uncover.

Five Subversion upgrades let you reboot fallen machines as temporary blue allies. Keep one stronger ally, or command a short-lived pair. Shoot charging junctions to interrupt hostile transmissions. Some things are better discovered inside the Annex.

Your gun is also your way out.

Shoot down. Go up. Recoil Foundry is a physics roguelike about building one absurd gun and surviving the factory around it. Every shot moves you. Every upgrade changes the next fight.

  • One gun, 105 upgrades. Branch into different firing styles and find combinations worth building a run around.
  • Twenty rooms. A new route after Furnace. Moving machinery, physical obstacles, changing layouts, and fights that punish standing still.
  • A reason to come back. A Daily Run with a shared seed and fixed upgrades, a Workshop for builds, unlockable Practice fights, and a Logbook full of factory records.

The complete browser release. Free to play, with future fixes and updates still possible. There are a few things best discovered yourself.

Controls

A / D or arrows to move · Space to jump · Mouse to aim · Left click to shoot · Right click for an equipped secondary · Esc / P to pause.

Shooting downward while airborne lifts you up. Start with Controls → Try controls if you want a safe place to learn. Keys, music, sound, and reduced effects are adjustable in Settings.

Progress & help

Designed for Windows desktop/laptop with keyboard and mouse. Browser support and known limitations.

Progress is saved in your browser. Use Settings → Progress to export a backup or restore one. Moving from the GitHub version? Export there, then import here; saves do not transfer between sites automatically.

Found a bug? Settings → Report an issue prepares the details for you to review. Or report it here. In the comments, tell me which build you would play again.
