Recoil Foundry 5.6 is live and free to play in your browser. Build one gun, turn its recoil into movement, and fight your way through a hostile factory. The official gameplay trailer shows the weapons, upgrade combinations and five of the bosses in action.

Watch the official gameplay trailer

What's in the current game?

  • 141 upgrades and six starting weapons to earn. Begin with the pistol and discover new tools and combinations as you play.
  • Twenty rooms across five factory zones. Changing encounters, branching jobs, optional hunts and alternate boss arenas give you more to explore.
  • A clearer next goal. Track unlocks, discover actual game artwork in the Logbook, and see the cosmetics you earn on reward cards.
  • More reasons to return. Daily Runs, Overtime, Security levels and the Classic Boss Gauntlet give you reasons to return.

New in 5.6: Remix Gauntlet

After your first Campaign victory and a remix discovery, turn your discovered boss arenas into a five-fight circuit. Carry health and upgrades between fights, choose a fitting or repair at each service stop, and try Overclocked for stronger bosses and warned counterattacks. Earn new cosmetics and share challenges with records for each gun, route and tier. Your existing progress stays in place.

Help shape the next update — a ten-minute playtest

I'm looking for 5–10 people who haven't played Recoil Foundry before. Try the normal game for about ten minutes, play however you normally would, and stop whenever you want. You don't need to win, record your screen or be good at roguelikes. Returning players are welcome too; just mention that you've played before. Keep your existing save.

After playing, leave a comment with three things:

  1. What was the best moment? A specific action, upgrade or encounter helps. Nothing standing out is useful feedback too.
  2. What first felt confusing, unfair or frustrating? What happened, and what did you expect?
  3. Did you want another run, and why? Say whether you actually restarted after your first death, tried but couldn't, stopped, or didn't die before you finished.

Add whether this was your first time, roughly how long you played, your browser and the room you reached if you remember. If you watched the trailer, read a guide or had someone help, mention that too. Honest stopping points are just as useful as a good run.

Prefer a form? Send a structured first-session report on GitHub. It requires a GitHub account; replying here only needs an itch.io account. For a bug, Feedback after a run or Pause → Report an issue → Copy details prepares the version, seed and build for you to review and share.

Playtest instructions. This round is open now with no deadline. Replies here and on GitHub are public; no names, email addresses, private files or recordings are requested. I'll use recurring friction to choose up to three focused fixes for a 5.6.1 polish update, with no patch date promised.

Thanks for trying it and helping me make the next run better.
