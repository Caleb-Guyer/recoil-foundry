# Better Drafts — 5.20.0

New Campaign upgrade choices consider the starting gun and owned fittings. A useful weapon or build follow-up sits alongside other legal directions, with a preference for different roles. The existing firing comparison still shows the complete gun before and after a choice.

The draft keeps the opening power safeguard, earned salvage, prerequisites, incompatible paths, unlock requirements and stage restrictions. A narrow legal pool can limit the available directions; it never unlocks an unavailable upgrade or offers an incompatible fitting just to fill a category.

## Hold one card

Choose **Hold for reroll** under an ordinary upgrade. The selected card stays in its original slot while **Reroll other two** replaces the others. Holding a second card moves the hold; selecting the held toggle again releases it. Holding does not choose or grant the upgrade.

The existing once-per-reward reroll still costs **12 health**, requires more than 12 health, or consumes an available free reroll credit. The original card and health cost survive Continue accurately; after rerolling, the spent state survives too. You still select only one upgrade. Holding alone costs nothing. If there are too few legal replacements, the reroll stays unavailable and the hold can still be released.

Earned salvage and repair cannot be held. Courier, Welder, Auditor and hunt rewards retain their special choice rules. Daily runs keep their fixed choice and have no held-card reroll.

## Fewer repeated choices

The game remembers up to nine distinct recently declined upgrades within the current factory zone. Unheld cards discarded by a reroll and unchosen cards on a completed reward enter that small history. The retained card is not declined by the reroll. The next draft prefers other legal choices; a useful weapon fit, opening strength or a small remaining pool can require a repeat. Moving into a different zone clears that history.

## Save and challenge compatibility

Start a **new Campaign** for Better Drafts. Campaign seed revision 100 owns the new draft rules. Existing Continue saves keep the cards and draft behavior recorded by their older seed. Daily rules, shared choices and scoring are unchanged. Existing unlocks and gun behavior are unchanged. New Campaign saves preserve their held card, spent reroll and bounded decline history; isolated tests never write player progress.

Overtime from a new Campaign keeps its new draft rules while retaining the existing Overtime arena revision. Old Campaigns entering Overtime retain their draft behavior.
