# Combat Flow — 5.16.0

Six familiar Campaign rooms now have authored tactical encounters: Overpass, Cargo Switchyard, Warm Exchange, Upper Return, Inspection Lane and Scrap Bridge. Each has three seeded compositions with different opening targets, enemy combinations and reinforcement order. Later fights pair support units with enemies they can actually help. Early Docks rooms retain a small, readable patrol roster.

Every encounter has an opening and two reserve groups. Reinforcements keep their visible arrival warning and entry protection. Recovery between groups creates time to cross the room, change height or reposition before the next commitment. Pressure cannot disappear indefinitely when a player stays near an entrance, and the room cannot award its upgrade while a group is still pending.

Existing ground routes and broad upper decks offer different attack angles. Breakable cover and explosive props create opportunities for precision, blast, chain and recoil builds. High and low route choices keep distinct layouts and enemy placements, including after Continue. There are no new combat instruction panels or extra HUD counters.

New Campaigns use revision **99**. The first room, special jobs, machine and support introductions, optional hunts, security encounters, Annex routes and other authored objectives keep their own designs. The final three rooftops and the Reclaimer retain the 5.15.0 Final Approach update. Existing Continue saves and pinned Dailies retain their recorded encounter rules. Overtime and boss Practice score rules are unchanged.

## Verification

Regression checks cover deterministic compositions, supported spawn hulls, prop clearance, mirrored routes, ordinary movement and recoil landings, actual route rewards and Continue, authored group order, complete arrival tells, active-enemy caps, blocked entrances, pause and hit stop, final-clear gating, and private death rematches. Legacy rooms and special encounters retain their existing wave rules.

The combat audit uses ordinary movement, jumping and shooting with real weapon damage, enemy AI, health and physics. It compares all six native starting weapons and representative legal upgraded builds. Its results are automated reachability and stability checks, not observed human playtests or a player win-rate estimate.

The final local regression suite passed all **3,264 tests**, and the production TypeScript/Vite build passed. The final ordinary-input audit completed **310 trials**: 308 clears and two combat deaths, with zero timeouts, invalid physics, blocked geometry, incomplete clears, weapon mutations or progress writes. All 22 legal upgraded-build trials cleared. All 48 geometry cases and 18 native-renderer captures were checked. The audit records hashes of the exact tested source.
