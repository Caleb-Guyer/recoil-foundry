# The Reclaimer — 5.14.0

The finale now tells its story through action. A playable walk through rain leads to a corrupted double boarding its first chassis. Destroy it and a giant transport retrieves the pilot and lowers the Siege. Destroy the Siege and the pilot leaps into the background, opens the city core and boards Rupture while you can still move and jump.

There are no cutscene captions, phase cards or explanatory overlays. The combat health bar is a red rectangle labeled **The Reclaimer**. Each machine has its own complete health bar; reaching zero starts its transition, clears delayed attacks, and restores the next form to full health. Only the final machine awards the boss kill.

The opening uses rain alone. Machine activation, aircraft engines, winch, landing, reactor and final explosion have original sound cues; the combat score begins when the first machine becomes active and escalates with the fight. Pausing, leaving the page or muting effects cleans up rain and scheduled cinematic sounds. Reduced effects preserves the acting and warnings with less flash and camera motion. The Logbook photographs the same native chassis drawing used in the fight.

## Combat and endings

The first chassis can draw from all nineteen weapon patterns after its opening. The Siege and Rupture use their own larger physical hulls and linked attack sequences without the small rival's recoil vaults. Heavy volleys and late crossfire gain followups. Each form has a larger health pool, stronger ammunition and more frequent arena strikes; New Game+ increases these further. Warnings keep their full timing, arena marks lock once, and every arena attack leaves a route out. The base floor remains intact as decks change.

The core-opening transition keeps movement and jumping active. Weapons and damage wait until Rupture is ready. Locked movie shots pause the run timer; the playable approach and core sequence count as gameplay.

Your first Campaign victory shows the Reclaimer's destruction. That run's extraction has no New Game+ door. After finishing, a once-only popup explains that **Overtime / New Game+ is available on future Campaign runs**, then continues to the existing pictured rewards. Later Campaign victories show the pilot escaping; taking the Overtime door lets you pursue him and see the final destruction after beating New Game+.

The ending choice is captured when the run starts and stored with Continue. A first victory cannot change its own extraction by unlocking the Shotgun mid-run. Death Retry preserves the choice; Again after a victory reads the cleared profile for the new run. Campaign revision **97** owns this fight. Earlier Campaigns, Dailies and revision-95/96 test links keep their rules. Earlier earned Practice identities remain available; current Practice uses score rules **6**.
