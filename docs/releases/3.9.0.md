# Recoil Foundry 3.9.0 — Melt-through

The Welder's cutting head is yours to salvage.

Defeating The Welder now guarantees **Melt-through** as one of its bonus upgrade choices. Take it or choose another upgrade; the regular room reward still follows. Each shot can cross one thin wall or platform, retaining 65% damage. Both faces briefly glow white-hot. Thick cover, machinery, crates and the edges of the room remain solid.

Two mutually exclusive follow-ups change what comes out the other side:

- **Clean Cut:** cross twice as much material and retain 85% damage.
- **Blowout:** the exit releases three short-range molten fragments alongside the surviving shot.

Bullets, volleys, beams, shells and steel balls share the same one-wall limit. Beams spend range inside the wall; explosive rounds retain reduced blast damage. Portals, banks and returning shots cannot refresh the passage. Blowout fragments cannot produce more fragments or melt through another wall.

The three upgrades have their own factory records and icons. Continue preserves pending choices, including Welder rewards saved before this update. Existing maximal-build links retain their previous upgrades. There is no new HUD.
