# Recoil Foundry 2.98.1

24 September 2026 · Browser bug fixes

[Play](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?v=2.98.1)

- **Blackout progression:** the electrical box stays anchored when a conveyor runs underneath it. Destroying nearby footing cannot turn it into a falling sensor. Shooting it still restores power and releases the reserve wave. Resolves [#1](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/1).
- **Upgrade menu:** repeated Escape presses cannot dismiss a pending reward and strand the run. Unexpected native dialog closures recover the active menu. Choosing, rerolling, backing out of other menus and pausing still work. Resolves [#2](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/2).
- **Boss cover stalls:** the Crane stows its hammer clear of its flak volley before warning and firing. When adjacent cover obscures every normal firing angle, shared boss navigation seeks a shot at the player's exposed edge. Resolves the reproduced Crane and Turbine stalls behind [#3](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/3). Attack warnings and solid cover remain in effect.

Existing saves and Daily identities remain compatible. This patch corrects blocked attacks and progression without changing upgrade pools, layouts, damage, health or warning durations.

Regression coverage includes the reported cooling high route and belt, repeated/native dialog dismissal, sustained Crane pressure in both mirrors and phases, and the Turbine's crate-side blind spot. Browser/device support and previously documented [limitations](../browser-support.md) are unchanged.

Validation: all **1,571 tests pass**; TypeScript and the production build pass. The upgrade-menu fix was also exercised in the browser with repeated Escape presses, rerolling, choosing a reward and pausing afterward.
