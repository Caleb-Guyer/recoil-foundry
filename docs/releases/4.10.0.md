# Boss Remixes — 4.10.0

After your first Campaign victory, fresh Campaigns can encounter two authored variants each of the Loader, Press and Condenser. Each variant changes its arena, machinery and attack sequence. Boss health stays at the normal stage value. Alternate Crane, Kiln and Turbine encounters keep their own arenas.

| Boss      | Arena         | What changes                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Loader    | Crossdock     | Three cargo braces and hanging loads. Rams alternate with warned fans; destroying a brace releases its load.                                  |
| Loader    | Switchyard    | A moving transfer lift between two braces. Two rams lead into a warned fan, with longer exposed recovery after a crash.                       |
| Press     | Split Die     | Divided ground cover and two steam lanes. Slams alternate with warned fans; shooting a ready valve can expose the press.                      |
| Press     | Stamping Line | Four low decks and one central steam lane. Two slams lead into a warned fan, rewarding changes of height.                                     |
| Condenser | Cold Circuit  | Two narrow coolant pools, cover and a central valve. Two single aimed volleys lead into one purge with four gaps.                             |
| Condenser | Purge Chamber | Dry decks across coolant and two valves. Its paired purges have four wide gaps that shift between volleys; the follow-up has its own warning. |

Attack warnings use the same trajectories as live shots. Fans and purges leave an exposed recovery; steam valves open armor without cancelling an attack. Machinery uses the existing physical collision, cargo, lift and pressure systems. Pause names the current arena and explains its mechanic.

Continue saves and Recent-run replays preserve the remix rules and seed. Retry of an enabled Campaign keeps the seed so its boss variants stay the same. Older Continue saves and old replays retain their original fights. First Campaigns, Daily, jobs, Overtime, Workshop and ordinary boss Practice keep their encounter schedules.

Encounter a live variant during an eligible Campaign to reveal it in **Practice → Boss remixes**. Unseen variants remain hidden. Cards show pictures rendered from the actual arenas. Use the existing preset or an owned Workshop build, save separate times and hit records for each variant/build, and share its RFC1 challenge code. Progress backups and Undo include discovered variants; older backups still import.
