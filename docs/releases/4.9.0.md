# Machine Teamwork — 4.9.0

Fresh Campaigns can introduce **Repair Drones** from room 7 in Furnace and **Relay Units** from room 11 in Cooling. Later patrols vary which support pair they contain. Placement uses existing safe patrol anchors with a nearby partner and an unobstructed connection. Rooms without a safe pair keep their ordinary roster. Only one patrol member is replaced; no extra enemies or combat menus are added. The pair enters the same wave, and introduction fights open with the pair before other patrols arrive.

Repair Drones show a dashed green tether and a cross over a wounded machine. After a **1.25-second warning**, they deliver pulses of up to **8 health**, never exceeding the target's maximum. Each drone carries **48 health** of compound for the entire room. Shooting across its exposed tether interrupts delivery for 3.5 seconds. Defeating the drone ends it. It cannot repair itself, another support unit, a boss, an allied/rebooted machine, an elite or a coordinated squad.

Relay Units use an amber tether and a lightning mark. After the same warning, one connected patrol's next native projectile gains **30% damage** and an amber outline and light. The normal attack warning, projectile speed and volley count stay intact. Only one projectile receives the boost. Each unit has **three charges**; an unused charge expires after **2.2 seconds** and disconnects. Cut the tether, destroy the unit or break the connection with cover before firing to discharge it.

Only one support connection can operate at a time. Range, real physical cover, target death, freezing, carrying and reboots interrupt it. Friendly bullets and beam pulses cut only along their actual travelled path, respecting scenery and portal transitions. Enemy rounds do not cut friendly support links. Both new machines have ordinary colliding physical bodies. Reduced effects keeps the tether, cross/lightning marks and charge outline visible without requiring pulsing effects.

Encountering each unit reveals its actual game artwork, behavior guide and maintenance record in the Logbook. Warning sounds distinguish repair and relay connections. Their ordinary patrol roles do not create a new progression currency or reward.

Campaign checkpoints save teamwork rules, and Recent-run seed replays retain them. Existing Continue saves and older seed replays keep their original rosters. Daily, boss Practice, Gauntlet, Workshop, Overtime, jobs, bosses, quiet post-boss rooms, Factory events and other special introductions keep their encounters. The opening zone has neither support unit.

Isolated development links use `?test=teamwork&unit=repair` or `?test=teamwork&unit=relay`, with optional `&gun=shotgun` or `&gun=nailgun` and `&v=1`. These use actual Campaign layouts and normal controls without writing progress or awarding discoveries.
