# Remix Gauntlet — 5.6.0

Your boss discoveries now form a lasting five-fight challenge. After your first Campaign victory and encountering at least one remix, open **Practice → Boss Gauntlet → Route: Remix**. The existing Classic route remains available.

## Build a circuit

Fight Loading, Furnace, Coolant, Reclamation and Command in order. Each department offers the remixes you have discovered, pictured with actual rendered arena photographs. Departments with no discoveries supply one standard fight. Discovering more arenas in Campaign expands your choices; the ten remixes provide 32 complete routes.

Start with an earned, unmodified gun and full health. Health carries forward; between rounds choose one offered upgrade or a repair of up to 30 health. You have four service stops. Remix boss health accounts for the arena movement and limited fittings. Only active combat counts toward the record; menus and pauses stop the clock. Restart begins the entire circuit again.

**Standard** keeps each arena’s ordinary warnings and recovery. **Overclocked** increases boss health by 15% and adds counter-volleys during alternating recoveries. Each counter gets a visible 1.05-second warning, locks its aim for the final 0.45 seconds, and leaves an opening afterward. Every other recovery stays uninterrupted. Classic combat rules are unchanged.

## Earn and share

| Achievement     | Requirement                                         | Reward                |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Full Circuit    | Complete five Remix Gauntlet fights on either tier  | Circuit Runner outfit |
| Self-Sufficient | Complete a Remix Gauntlet without choosing a repair | Cold Steel gun finish |

Fitting-based healing is allowed for Self-Sufficient. Newly earned rewards appear as actual appearance cards on the result screen and use individual **New** badges in Appearance. Once a remix is discovered, both achievements appear in the Logbook and can be tracked as your next goal. Classic completions do not fulfill these goals. A first Gauntlet completion can also earn the existing Victor outfit and Pressure repeater license.

Remix records compare the same gun, five arenas and tier, preserving Classic records. From a completed result or route record, **Share challenge** copies a link or `RFG1.` code. Import it through the Gauntlet setup to preview its target time, hit count and fixed route. Friends choose their own upgrades or repairs; the gun, tier and arenas stay fixed. They must earn the gun, clear Campaign and discover each required remix before entering. Links do not reveal locked arenas or grant progress.

Gauntlet records, discoveries, rewards and tracked goals use the existing backup/restore and Undo system. Campaign Continue remains available; Gauntlet fights do not award Campaign boss victories or clean-arena masteries.
