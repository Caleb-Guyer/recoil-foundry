# Choose Your Goal — 5.4.0

After your first Campaign victory, choose **Track this** on an incomplete, revealed Logbook weapon, achievement or fitting. The existing title card becomes **Tracked goal** and follows your choice, so you can work toward the Pressure repeater before finishing the Coil carbine's hunt victories.

Your choice uses the existing run goal, opportunity cues, upgrade prerequisites and end-of-run progress. Goal details still open the appropriate unlocked setup. The original goal stays attached to an attempt already underway: a new selection during Pause applies to the next attempt, and Continue preserves its original baseline. Retry uses your latest choice.

**Stop tracking** in the Logbook or **Use automatic goals** in title goal details returns to the regular suggestions. Completing the goal does the same automatically. There is no extra menu or permanent combat panel.

## Reveal and save rules

- Beat the Campaign stays the goal until the first victory, including after failed first attempts.
- Unknown upgrades and concealed achievements cannot be tracked or revealed by importing a preference. An encountered upgrade's record can reveal its related challenge, just as the Logbook does; seeing an offer still does not count as collecting it.
- Named fitting licenses track their unlock requirement first. After the license is earned, the named fitting can be tracked for collection even before its full record is encountered.
- Completed weapons, earned achievements and collected fittings have no tracking action. Tracking never grants a reward or changes a draft.
- Your choice is included in progress backup, restore and undo. Older profiles migrate to automatic goals. Completed or unavailable choices fall back to the actual profile's automatic suggestion.
- Daily keeps its existing goal display rules. Ordinary isolated playtests and sample Logbooks cannot change your saved goal.
