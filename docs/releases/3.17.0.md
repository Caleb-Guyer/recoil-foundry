# 3.17.0 — Weapon Mastery

Three new feats reward creative gunplay with permanent cosmetic finishes and recovered factory reports:

- **Bank Job → Carom.** Defeat a boss with at least half your credited damage coming from ricocheted shots. Green enamel and a brass corner mark; a toolroom claim from M. Vale. Appears after discovering Bank shot.
- **Air Traffic → Airmail.** Defeat six enemies in one room without landing between kills. Beacon blue with a pale flight stripe; an air-movement report from Dispatch. Appears after discovering Kickback or Airshot.
- **Special Delivery → Waybill.** Defeat four enemies in one room with shots that traveled through your portals. Violet metal with a cream routing label; a Development routing exception. Appears after discovering Fold.

Requirements live in **Logbook → Commendations**. Earned finishes equip in **Workshop → Appearance**. A brief notification waits for combat to clear; rewards also appear in the run recap. No combat checklist, permanent stat bonus or new currency is added.

Damage attribution uses actual health lost after armor, excluding overkill. Shell payloads, splinters, surface saws and beam segments retain the path that produced them. Reflected hostile rounds and independent secondary effects do not borrow a gunshot's path. Ground contact resets the airborne streak, including a landing between kills. Boss cleanup, allied fire, targets still arriving, relay devices, sentries and repeated deaths cannot pad the counters.

Campaign, Security, Daily and Overtime runs can earn rewards. Practice, Workshop and isolated previews cannot. Partial challenge evidence resets with its room; Continue restarts that room's attempt. Earned finishes and reports persist through existing progress backups. Combat stats, encounter selection and Daily/Practice rule versions are unchanged.
