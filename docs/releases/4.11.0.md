# Rare Miniboss Hunts — 4.11.0

New Campaigns have a 50% chance to schedule one optional hunt in zones two through five. Clear the patrol and jump at the marked side door to accept. Taking the main exit skips the assignment. Special rooms keep their own entrances; a hunt never crowds a job, blackout, shaft or escape route.

- **The Cableweaver:** warned live cables, shootable terminals and a recovery opening when a cable breaks.
- **The Bulwark:** solid movable shield plates and an elevated cannon. Shoot the plates away or attack the exposed core from above. Only two sets of plates are available.
- **The Demolisher:** planted explosives with a visible blast radius and fuse. Shoot a charge to disarm it; solid cover blocks its blast. The machine carries six charges.

Each machine has a separate, spacious arena. Its hazards stop when it dies. Every attack keeps its warning, including volleys used after supplies run out.

## Rewards and Practice

A successful Campaign hunt offers **30 health** or **one free upgrade reroll**. You return to the cleared patrol with the same upgrades and room number. First victories also earn Copperline, Sentinel or Fusekeeper, displayed on appearance reward cards, and unlock that machine in Practice. The Practice list and Logbook use the actual gameplay renderer for their portraits. Practice records and challenges track each machine separately.

Continue preserves the selected hunt, an unfinished fight, a pending reward and an unused reroll. Retry keeps the Campaign seed and hunt schedule. Older saves, Dailies, Workshop, Gauntlet and other isolated tests retain their previous rules.
