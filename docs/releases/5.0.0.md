# Toolroom Expansion — 5.0.0

Recoil Foundry now has six starting tools, 141 upgrades, three new machine roles with six behavioral variants, and nine authored later-zone layouts. The twenty-room Campaign keeps its calm opening and introduces the new machinery progressively.

## Earn a different starting tool

| Tool              | How it plays                                             | Unlock                                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Service pistol    | Accurate single rounds and balanced recoil               | Standard issue                                 |
| Recoil shotgun    | Five pellets and large recoil kicks                      | Beat the Campaign                              |
| Burst nailgun     | Three quick nails and smaller kicks                      | Complete Overtime                              |
| Twinbore          | Two tight rounds and a deliberate firing cycle           | Defeat the Press or Kiln in Campaign or Daily  |
| Coil carbine      | Heavy, fast rounds that penetrate one additional enemy   | Complete two different Campaign miniboss hunts |
| Pressure repeater | Continuous light rounds with frequent small recoil kicks | Complete the Boss Gauntlet                     |

The first playthrough still starts automatically with the pistol. After it, the equipment picker shows the other tools with their requirements and actual gun artwork. Existing victories and achievements count toward the new licenses; no need to repeat an accomplishment already saved.

Every tool accepts the same fitting system. Twinbore retains its tight central pair inside Scattershot's wider fan. Beam conversions keep separate pellets and firing lanes, while penetration, stored charges, recoil and cadence retain their effects. Native carbine and repeater ammunition also have distinct lighting and procedural firing sounds; conversions keep the light of their ammunition.

## Twenty-four earned fittings

Each accomplishment opens four fittings for subsequent Campaign drafts. They begin appearing after the first zone, and their normal prerequisites still apply. Unlocking a fitting permits offers; discovering or taking it retains the usual Logbook and Workshop progression.

| Family and requirement                                    | New fittings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Movement: defeat the Loader or Crane                      | **Spring Step:** stronger extra air jump. **Glide Rig:** two seconds of glide per landing. **Impulse Reserve:** stronger first airborne recoil. **Ground Anchor:** less grounded kick.                                                                       |
| Precision: defeat the Press or Kiln                       | **Surveyor:** mark targets for later discharges. **Far Sight:** stronger direct fire at range. **Follow the Mark:** marked kills prime the next discharge. **Stagger Coil:** charged hits delay an idle ordinary machine.                                    |
| Heat: defeat the Condenser, Turbine or Switchboard        | **Heat Exchanger:** carry 75% heat. **Insulated Line:** keep the transfer for 1.8 seconds. **Thermal Budget:** build tracked heat faster. **Hot Start:** start the room's first exposed target with heat.                                                    |
| Banking: defeat three different main bosses               | **Bank Capacitor:** increase maximum payout to 75%. **Bank Memory:** retain a quarter of the reserve after spending it. **Kinetic Liner:** an extra surface bank with lighter damage. **Guide Vane:** faster rounds and longer beams.                        |
| Defense: defeat the Sorter                                | **Reinforced Plate:** absorb heavier ordinary rounds. **Plate Retainer:** keep a plate for six seconds. **Reclamation:** recover limited health from directly destroyed cover/crates. **Field Patch:** limited recovery after avoiding injury during combat. |
| Cadence: complete the Campaign escape or factory shutdown | **Opening Shot:** reward pauses between discharges. **Recoil Runner:** stronger fire while moving quickly. **Clean Cycle:** two quick direct kills prime a discharge. **Regulator:** reduce recoil with slightly lighter damage.                             |

Marks, stored damage and conditional bonuses belong to a whole discharge. Wide pellet and beam patterns cannot multiply their payout. Combined cadence bonuses cap at 40%; boosted Overkill payouts cannot refill themselves. Healing caps at ten health per room for each repair fitting, and Field Patch stops after combat. Stagger Coil preserves committed warnings and boss attacks.

Upgrade comparisons use real physics and targets, including heat transfer, stronger armor, retained plates and repairs. The final build report accounts for the new transfer and banking values. Large unlock rewards show the first six pictured cards with the remainder available in an expandable list; every reward remains individually recorded and readable.

## New patrol machines and spaces

- **Shutter Guard:** its closed front shutter absorbs most direct fire. Flank it, or attack when the core opens near the end of its warning and during recovery. Fan and Burst guards change the firing pattern; the Burst guard warns before each shot.
- **Strider:** patrols its assigned lane, then braces and commits to aimed fire. Survey and Volley variants change its warning, movement and pattern.
- **Mortar Cart:** warns a real parabolic shell path and landing blast. Shoot the shell in flight or use physical cover. Twin and Slow variants change landing areas and timing. Cover blocks explosions, and destroying the owner removes its remaining shells.

Nine roomy layouts support heat chains, overkill banking and pushes between breakable barricades: Relay aisle, Warm exchange, Upper return, Inspection lane, Die store, Return chute, Cover line, Scrap bridge and Roof braces. New Campaign and Daily seeds can use them in later zones. First encounters introduce a base machine alone before the later patrol arrives; variants begin later. Special jobs, detours and other authored encounters retain their space.

The Logbook photographs the actual new enemy variants and rooms with the game renderer. Each variant and place has its own discovery notice. Evacuation jobs in new Campaign runs include a visible two-second boarding hold on their real support; leaving the marked deck resets boarding.
