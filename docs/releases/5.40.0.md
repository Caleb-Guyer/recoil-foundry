# 5.40.0 — Surface Machines

The Angler and Wallcrawler gain working mechanical rigs, local hit reactions, health-based wear, distinct shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage.

## Working surface machinery

- **Angler:** an articulated stance braces a bank-lock gimbal and receiver around the real recorded bank shot. Its casing opens during the actual interruption/exposure window.
- **Wallcrawler:** gripping legs follow the actual supporting face. Individual shots, lost grip, falling vulnerability and recovery have distinct mechanical poses.

Attack paths, recorded muzzles, shot timing, native damage and support-breaking counterplay retain their rules. Wreckage uses detached snapshots without adding collision bodies or rewards. Pause and Reduced Effects are supported.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and reaction courses use the selected starting tool; shutdown and wreckage courses supply an upgraded tool. A distant guard keeps the room active while shutdowns settle. These private courses preserve Continue, unlocks, discoveries and records.

## Real surface counterplay

- [Angler bank interruption](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=surface-machines&unit=angler&scene=mechanisms&v=1): shoot the marked overhead bank surface during its warning. Losing the actual face interrupts the shot and exposes the casing.
- [Wallcrawler grip interruption](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=surface-machines&unit=wallcrawler&scene=mechanisms&v=1): shoot its marked support to cancel the remaining burst and expose the falling hull.
- Wallcrawler mounting comparisons: [Floor](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=surface-machines&unit=wallcrawler&scene=animations&face=floor&v=1), [Wall](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=surface-machines&unit=wallcrawler&scene=animations&face=wall&v=1), [Ceiling](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=surface-machines&unit=wallcrawler&scene=animations&face=ceiling&v=1).

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` changes the private tool. Wallcrawler supports optional `face=floor`, `face=wall` or `face=ceiling`. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes, Angler face overrides and unsupported link versions fail closed.
