# 5.25.0 — Uprising Remaster

The eleven optional Factory Uprising jobs now have distinct sequences and authored patrols, counterattacks and pursuit groups. Moving cargo, convoy defense, research interlocks, sequential crew stations and real lowered service bridges replace repeated short objective loops. Arrivals use native enemy behavior and damage, respect a bounded roster and show their normal warning before entering.

Success earns **one job payment** before the ordinary room upgrade: recover up to **20 health**, bank a **free reroll**, or compare and confirm a **legal refit**. A refit exchanges one fitting while preserving the gun's prerequisites, firing mechanism and earned combinations. The complete before/after firing range shows what changes. Reroll credits survive Continue and can carry into earned New Game+; every payment is recorded and spent once. Repair is unavailable at full health.

Job selection keeps the current choices in view and places the campaign map behind one expandable control. The objective and later consequence appear together; completed jobs record the chosen payment in the map. World markers distinguish the active station, interlocks, cargo and the exact boarding deck without adding another HUD panel. Reduced Effects keeps the same physical markers and warning boundaries.

**Core sabotage fix:** successful sabotage keeps the Turbine's updraft shafts disabled through every phase and Continue, including existing revision-104 campaigns. Previously, a later boss phase could incorrectly restore them. Other successful job effects retain their native pursuit, crew cover and final security-response rules.

## Compatibility

Start a **new Campaign** for the remastered jobs (Campaign revision 105). The first decision stays after room 5; the jobs do not appear in the opening zone. Existing Continue saves, older seed layouts, recorded Practice, Daily and earlier Overtime identities keep their existing mission and reward rules. Earlier jobs still restore 8 health on success. New Game+ does not introduce another set of jobs.

Pending job payments save with their original build and unlock pool. They cannot be rerolled, held, duplicated or silently reapplied on Continue. A completed historical outcome without a payment receipt never acquires a new payment. Test links use isolated fixtures and do not modify your Continue, unlocks or collection.
