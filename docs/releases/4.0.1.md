# Factory Uprising room and interface pass — 4.0.1

The next-job screen now puts the decision first: two or three compact cards show the objective and its consequence. The health bonus appears once. The campaign map, finale status and contract hint live inside **Campaign progress**, collapsed until opened. Keyboard shortcuts and controller menu navigation continue to use the same choices. Mission HUD instructions no longer repeat a generic paragraph in the district banner.

All eleven jobs now have their own authored geometry, objective placement, cover and approach route. Prototype cases and sabotage relays occupy supported raised stations, giving movement and firing angles a purpose. Defense rooms leave an open firing lane around the generator, with physical cover at its sides and clear, warned reinforcement sites. Security squads remain present in job rooms.

**Last train out** and **Rooftop evacuation** require opening two elevated route switches in order by jumping beside them, then landing on the marked boarding platform within forty seconds. Running along the floor to the room door cannot complete the objective. The boarding zone belongs to the mission, has its own physical support and sits clear of the normal exit, upper-route door and reserved exit steps. Its marker disappears when the job resolves and cannot carry into another room or final extraction.

Moving cars keep their full travel clear of fixed platforms. Powered Core platforms retain standing clearance after sabotage. Fixed objective stations keep cases and relays from clipping into moving machinery. Generators sit on the floor, and resolved objectives are removed from both collision and drawing. The existing abandon action remains available through Pause; missing a job does not block its normal exit.

Saved Campaign records, contracts, completed objectives and old non-Uprising saves retain their schema. Continuing an unfinished job restarts that room's switches and timer, as it does for its patrol and other transient machinery.
