# Ghost Races — 4.7.0

**Practice → Recoil Trials** now offers optional races against your own recorded best. A new unmodified Practice best saves the movement path for that course and starting gun. Race my ghost controls the translucent replay and personal checkpoint comparisons. Ghosts use the actual player and gun artwork; they cannot collide, fire real bullets, damage targets or affect your movement. Checkpoint comparisons appear briefly above the player. Pause and hitstop freeze the replay and trial clock together.

Each of the three courses keeps one ghost per starting gun. Faster clears replace it; equal times prefer fewer shots. Existing times remain, and earning a new Practice best supplies their recording. Paths are limited to three minutes and 2,048 samples per clear. Longer clears still retain their time records and can share their checkpoint targets. Recordings are stored compactly in progress backups; older backups migrate to an empty ghost collection. Restore and Undo restore include the recordings.

**Share** beside a recorded course, or **Share this clear** after a successful Practice run, opens a challenge card with a code and link. The code includes only the course, starting gun, rules version, final time, shot count and checkpoint times. A friend can import either in Practice → Recoil Trials on GitHub Pages or itch.io. Share links open a preview before starting. The course must have been cleared in Campaign and the gun must be unlocked. Full health and no upgrades keep the starting conditions consistent. Retry preserves the challenge.

Shared times are supplied by their sender, rather than verified competition results. The ghost shown during a shared challenge remains your own matching recording; split comparisons use the shared target. Unsupported, malformed or mixed-mode codes are rejected without changing progress. Campaign, Daily, Workshop, Overtime, weapon unlocks and trial mastery keep their existing rules.
