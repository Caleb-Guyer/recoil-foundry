# 5.36.0 — Heavy Industry

Shutter Guards, Striders and Mortar Carts now have working mechanical rigs, body-local hit reactions, distinct shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage. Their actual combat states drive the presentation.

## Read the mechanism

The Shutter Guard’s moving armor follows its real frontal protection and exposed firing window. Wide and Cycle Shutters retain their separate fan and two-shot patterns. The artwork does not grant a new weak-point bonus.

The Strider’s articulated legs follow actual motion and support. Its braces, receiver and variant barrels communicate tracking, committed firing lanes and recovery. A stationary machine does not walk in place. Survey and Volley Striders retain their native warnings and attacks.

The Mortar Cart’s loading and barrel motion follow its planned ballistic arcs, actual release and recovery. Twin and Heavy Mortars keep their own shell patterns. The existing landing marks, cover predictions, shootable shells and blast behavior remain in place.

## Damage and shutdown

Accepted hits produce local armor deflections or real health-loss reactions. Mechanical wear follows actual health. Each defeated family has its own failure: shutter drive and panels, Strider supports and carriage, or Mortar Cart drive and barrel. Defeated variants remain recognizable without active warnings.

The existing bounded contact and wreck pipeline handles real supported settling, moving supports, Pause, room reset and Reduced Effects. Wrecks have no collision bodies. Bosses, targets and cleanup removals retain their existing presentation. No AI, damage, collision, audio or save migration is introduced.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and hit-reaction courses use the chosen starting tool. Shutdown and wreckage courses supply an upgraded tool; the latter begin on an actual elevated deck. A distant real guard keeps an early shutdown visible before room clear. All tests preserve Continue, discoveries, unlocks and saved records.
