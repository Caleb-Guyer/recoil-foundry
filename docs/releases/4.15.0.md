# Support Masteries — 4.15.0

Three new commendations turn the support upgrades into single-run goals:

| Challenge            | Complete in one Campaign or Daily run     | Appearance reward    |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Relay Race           | Six completed Heat Relay transfers        | Heatline gun finish  |
| Stored Energy        | Eight Overkill Bank charges spent         | Reservoir gun finish |
| Reclaimed Protection | Five enemy bullets blocked by Scrap Armor | Patchwork outfit     |

Heatline has warm red enamel and a pale transfer line. Reservoir uses violet enamel and brass cell markings. Patchwork joins olive workwear with blue replacement panels, pale patches and visible fasteners. All three use the gameplay renderer in the Logbook, Appearance and end-of-run reward cards. They preserve weapon behavior, player silhouette and readable indicators.

Challenges reveal when their upgrade is encountered, including an upgrade offer. Unseen challenges conceal their names, goals and rewards. The Logbook shows a current or Continue attempt's progress; completed commendations stay completed. The run report's closed Recorded results panel shows relevant mastery targets alongside the existing counters. No new HUD notices are added.

Only completed transfers, actual positive reserve payments and real plate absorption count. A pellet volley or beam pattern counts as one Overkill payment. Collecting a plate, heavy ammunition, hazards, missed relays and invulnerability do not create progress. Targets award immediately on reaching their threshold, once per profile. The end screen retains newly earned cards even if the run ends in defeat. Equipping remains the player's choice.

Room changes and Continue keep the same attempt's counters. A fresh run or Retry starts at zero. Qualifying counters in an unfinished 4.14 Continue save are recognized when play resumes. Older saves without counters begin a marked partial record; earlier progress is not invented. Opening old recaps does not grant rewards or combine attempts. Existing backup data carries the new commendations, cosmetics, reward ledger and counters without a new save version. Practice, Workshop and preset tests cannot earn these rewards.
