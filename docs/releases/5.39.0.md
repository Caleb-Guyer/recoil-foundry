# 5.39.0 — District Machines

Borer, Sifter and Skimmer machinery now follows actual movement, aiming and admitted shots, with local damage reactions, distinct shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage.

## Working district machinery

- **Borer:** working tracks, real cover bracing and a compact receiver follow its native three-round volley.
- **Sifter:** a banking hover rig works its fans and opens firing ports around the actual wide volley.
- **Skimmer:** the impeller, louvers and receiver distinguish tracking, committed aim and real discharge.

Accepted hull hits create local damage reactions; casing wear follows actual health. Borer drives fail, Sifter fans and fins lose support, and Skimmer impellers coast down into unlit wrecks. Wreckage settles toward actual support without adding collision bodies or rewards. Existing shots, warnings, damage, timing, physics and saved progress keep their rules. Rendering uses detached bounded snapshots and never advances the simulation.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and reaction courses use the selected starting tool; shutdown and wreckage courses supply an upgraded tool. Wreckage courses place the player on a real raised deck, with flying subjects above it. A distant guard keeps the room active while shutdowns settle. These tests preserve Continue, unlocks, discoveries and records.
