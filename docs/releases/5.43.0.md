# 5.43.0 — Factory Overseers

The Welder, Switchboard and Auditor gain working machinery, local damage reactions, health-based wear and distinct mechanical shutdowns with brief inert wreckage.

- **Welder:** tracked movement, an articulated torch, connected hoses and cooling shutters follow actual seam, barricade and arc attacks and the existing exposed recovery window.
- **Switchboard:** three physical channel mechanisms distinguish real recording, target lock, transmission and junction interruptions. The existing arena ports and warnings remain authoritative.
- **Auditor:** planted movement, braced receiver cycles and committed charges follow its actual attacks. A living recall folds the working rig for retreat; an actual defeat has its own shutdown and wreck.

Accepted hull hits produce local reactions and casing wear. Wrecks retain the prelethal machine pose, lose their lights and settle toward real support. They cannot create terrain, shots, hazards, damage or collision. Native owner-specific gear and projectile cleanup retain their existing rules.

The short visible post-clear settling window keeps movement and Pause available and does not add its cosmetic time to the run record. Reduced Effects retains representative working states and steady defeated poses. Combat balance, targeting, attack timing, physics, rewards, saves and encounter selection retain their rules.

## Counterplay and recall

- [Welder counterplay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=overseers&unit=welder&scene=mechanisms&v=1): avoid warned seams, break temporary barricades and attack during its cooling opening.
- [Switchboard counterplay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=overseers&unit=switchboard&scene=mechanisms&v=1): move after lock and shoot the amber junction during charge to interrupt the real transmission.
- [Auditor counterplay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=overseers&unit=auditor&scene=mechanisms&v=1): evade committed charges and warned firing lanes while its real armor and recovery rules apply.
- [Auditor living recall](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=overseers&unit=auditor&scene=recall&v=1): survive its first 18-second visit to see it fold and retreat. Recall grants no kill, defeat reward or wreck.

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` selects the private starting tool. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes, fragments and unsupported link versions fail closed. Recall is an Auditor-only inspection.
