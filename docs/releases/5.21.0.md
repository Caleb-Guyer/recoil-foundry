# Choose Your Route — 5.21.0

Four middle-zone branches now offer distinct terrain and encounters. A small nearby-exit preview shows the room actually resolved by the game, and accepted route choices remain available in Campaign progress.

## Four remastered branches

- **Boiler tunnels:** sheltered firing lanes, overhead flanks, breakable cover and a valve-controlled pressure vent.
- **Flue bridges:** broad raised decks, exposed crossings, cover and an optional moving lift.
- **Intake channels:** ground cover, shallow coolant and a sheltered upper route.
- **Cooling towers:** elevated flanks and an optional fan-assisted crossing, with safe recovery below.

Each room keeps a traversable entrance, a clear exit approach and recoverable falls. A starting pistol can complete every route. Saved teamwork rules keep the Furnace Repairer and Cooling Relay introductions; the authored alternate encounter uses the existing reinforcement warnings. Special jobs and other reserved encounters retain their priority.

## See the route before committing

Clear the room and approach a high or low exit. One compact invitation shows the destination name, two factual terrain traits and a photograph made with the actual game renderer. Move away, open a menu or enter combat and the invitation hides. The current door symbols remain visible. The existing reward eyebrow identifies the selected high or low route while its upgrade is pending.

The destination is resolved through the full native room pipeline, including mirrors, security and job outcomes. The photograph hides enemies, the player and navigation markers; it does not invent a new room or reveal a future patrol. A completed sabotage refreshes the preview and removes machinery labels when that machinery is absent.

The optional preview view loads on demand. It retains at most two thumbnail photographs and one display canvas. Temporary physics worlds are released after resolving or photographing a destination. Looking at a preview does not tick the live simulation, consume the combat random stream, grant progress or write a checkpoint.

## Remember the chosen path

Pause and open **Campaign progress**, then expand **Routes taken**. Choices are recorded only after accepting the upgrade that commits the destination. Merely approaching a door or opening a reward does not record a route.

Up to five first-Campaign decisions survive Continue and progress backup/restore. Missing earlier history stays unknown. New Game+ keeps those original Campaign decisions and does not mix in a second lap.

## Compatibility

Start a **new Campaign** for Choose Your Route. Campaign revision 101 owns the remastered route geometry, invitations and saved history. Existing Continue saves retain their layouts and draft behavior. Daily rules and New Game+ layouts are unchanged. Better Drafts, weapon unlocks, upgrades, movement and damage rules are preserved.
