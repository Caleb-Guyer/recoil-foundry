# Scrap Circuit — 3.4.0

Overtime Reclamation now has six authored layouts built around moving, destructible scrap cover:

- **Scrap sorting floor:** paired delivery rails above separated firing positions.
- **Transfer gantries:** a high delivery crossing and a lower moving barrier.
- **Scrap service tunnels:** the lower route, with deliveries beneath long canopies.
- **Scrap highline:** the upper route, with staggered platforms and three timed loads.
- **Separation line:** the Sorter's arena, with two finite delivery traps.
- **Salvage vault:** the Reclaimer's arena, with a third overhead delivery.

Shoot a yellow latch to release a load early. Each load stops, gives a full 1.15-second warning with a flashing latch, falling dust, landing mark and three-part sound, then drops through normal physics. It becomes movable, breakable cover after landing. Bullets, Ray and continuous Torch use the same physical latch; terrain and other cover still block shots. Automatic drops give the same warning.

Each room has only two or three loads. A blocked trolley stops and warns before releasing instead of pushing players, enemies or props through obstacles. There is no repeating supply to farm. Remaining machinery powers down when the room clears. Permanent routes remain traversable with ordinary jumps after every load falls or breaks. Reduced effects retains the latch and landing warnings without flashing or moving dust.

New Overtime entries use revision 4, retaining the Docks, Furnace and Cooling remixes. Existing saves keep their original generation, including revision 3. Ordinary campaign, Daily, upgrades, Rooftops and earned boss Practice keep their rules. Practice ruleset remains 1. Rollbacks must preserve revision-4 checkpoint decoding and generation.
