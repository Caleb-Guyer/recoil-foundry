# 5.27.1 — Reclaimer pursuit

Fixes [issue #10](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/10): later Reclaimer forms could remain motionless and fire into cover, while successful sabotage removed their remaining arena pressure.

## Combat changes

- All three forms find reachable firing angles using their real collision hull. The Chassis pursues, Siege changes flanks and Rupture travels across the arena. Traversal uses the existing recoil-vault warning, physical collision and a fresh attack warning after moving.
- A blocked firing lane triggers repositioning. Committed warnings keep their muzzle position; moving the boss does not move a locked shot onto the player. Delayed payloads finish before traversal begins.
- Longer follow-up sequences and shorter breaks between linked attacks increase pressure. Full attack tells and recovery openings remain. Each form has a larger independent health pool; New Game+ adds faster traversal and stronger sequences.
- Siege and Rupture mark the player's position for a committed arena strike, including sheltered platforms. The mark stays fixed after its warning begins. Move out of the marked lane before it fires.
- Successful isolation still removes paired machinery and some arsenal attacks. The Reclaimer retains a single, slower arena mark with a longer warning. Sabotage remains useful without leaving a permanently safe firing spot.

This patch repairs **C107 Campaigns, including current 5.27.0 Continue saves**, and their New Game+ fights. Archived Campaign revisions through C106 and pinned Daily runs retain their recorded rules. Campaign generation, upgrade pools, unlocks, endings and cutscenes retain their existing rules.
