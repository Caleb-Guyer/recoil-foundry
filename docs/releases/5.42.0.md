# 5.42.0 — Contract Machines

The Cableweaver, Bulwark and Demolisher gain distinct working machinery, local damage reactions, health-based wear, mechanical shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage.

- **Cableweaver:** connected cable drums, brakes and an anchor launcher follow real windups, successful paired deployments, live cable tension and actual player cuts.
- **Bulwark:** planted hydraulic braces and a supported high receiver follow its real muzzle and shots. Shield hardware retains its protected state while any native plate survives; breaking both plates reveals the core.
- **Demolisher:** six charge cells, a transfer linkage and loading rack follow remaining native supplies, committed placement and actual disarms. Disarming a planted charge does not refund its spent inventory.

Accepted hull hits produce local reactions and casing wear. Cables lose drive, hydraulic supports collapse and charge loaders settle into unlit wrecks. Destroyed hunt gear follows its existing cleanup; the wreck cannot create cables, armor props, charges, damage or collision. A short visible shutdown window completes before the normal hunt result or reward screen, with movement and Pause still available. This settling time does not increase your recorded run time.

Native damage, armor, targeting, timing, physical projectiles, arena machinery, hunt rewards and saved progress retain their rules. Reduced Effects keeps representative mechanical states and steady defeated poses. The existing world warnings, cables, plates and charge indicators remain authoritative.

## Gear counterplay

- [Cableweaver](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=contract-machines&unit=cableweaver&scene=mechanisms&v=1): Jump live cables and shoot an anchor to cut the circuit. Watch the drums release tension during the actual interruption.
- [Bulwark](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=contract-machines&unit=bulwark&scene=mechanisms&v=1): Shots push and break the real shield plates. One surviving plate still protects the hull; break both or get above them to reach the core.
- [Demolisher](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=contract-machines&unit=demolisher&scene=mechanisms&v=1): Shoot planted charges to disarm them, or leave their marked blast radius before the fuse expires. The spent rack cell remains empty.

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` selects a different private starting tool. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes, fragments and unsupported link versions fail closed.
