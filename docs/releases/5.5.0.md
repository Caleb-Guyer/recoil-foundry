# Late Shift — 5.5.0

Four new boss arenas join fresh Campaigns after your first victory. Earlier Continue saves, seed replays and Daily retain their original fights.

- **Beltline:** opposing physical conveyors carry you across the Sorter’s locked induction lanes. Two field strikes lead into a wider fan; each release opens the housing.
- **Magnetic Return:** staggered magnets lift and drop real scrap crates. Split fans leave an opening around the aimed line, alternating with three warned induction lanes.
- **Skybridge:** two transfer lifts connect upper decks. Command alternates aimed pairs, broad fans and rings with four gaps. Changing height after the aim locks opens a route to retaliate.
- **Crossfire:** staggered platforms and paired fans with shifting gaps. Both releases receive a full warning; the core opens after the pair.

Factory Uprising consequences remain active. An isolated defense loses its rings, crew support removes follow-up volleys, overload accelerates the attack mix, and cargo pursuit brings the Interceptor into the new arena. That rival has a separate authored arsenal: Skybridge uses scatter fire and warned dives, while Crossfire pairs shifting fans with returning ammunition and warned echoes. Crew barricades still provide physical cover.

## Discover and master

Encounter an arena in an eligible Campaign to reveal its actual rendered photograph in **Logbook → Places**, its rematch in **Practice → Boss remixes**, and its mastery requirement. The six earlier remixes also have arena records; saved discoveries carry across.

Win a Campaign fight without taking damage to earn its cosmetic:

| Arena           | Achievement       | Reward               |
| --------------- | ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Beltline        | Between the Lines | Beltline gun finish  |
| Magnetic Return | Unattracted       | Lodestone gun finish |
| Skybridge       | Above the Orders  | Highrise outfit      |
| Crossfire       | Read Both Signals | Signalkeeper outfit  |

Each revealed achievement can be tracked as your next goal. Earned cosmetics use pictured run-reward cards and the existing individual **New** badges in Appearance. Taking damage remains recorded through Continue; restarting a damaged room cannot earn a clean-fight certificate. Practice and isolated tests grant no unlocks.

Final-room rematches support their full nineteen-fitting presets, custom builds, recorded times and challenge codes. Practice uses the arena’s standard Reclaimer sequence; job consequences apply in Campaign. Rematch records stay separate from ordinary Reclaimer fights.
