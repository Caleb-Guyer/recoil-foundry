# 5.23.0 — First Shift

The Loader, Crane, Press and Kiln now have distinct three-phase encounters and authored arenas in new Campaigns. Each starts with a readable pattern, then builds toward linked attacks with fresh warnings and exposed recovery windows. Native boss health, armor, weapon damage and rewards are retained.

- **Loader — Cargo breach:** committed rams develop into return rams and a ram/flak combination. The real 65% and 30% cargo failures retain their full warning, falling loads and destructible supports. Raised flanks and low landings offer different firing angles.
- **Crane — Hook gantry:** sweeps and slams develop into crosscuts and hook sequences. The physical head follows terrain-safe paths; warning polygons include its actual rectangular hull. A working counterweight and permanent decks offer routes above the floor.
- **Press — Stamping galleries:** locked crush columns and flak develop into separately warned stamp/flak/stamp sequences. Real shelves stop the press, and its return route works around overhead cover. The committed column bounds accepted crush damage, including after outside impulses.
- **Kiln — Cooling grates:** aimed flak, embers and supported heat grates combine into linked pressure. Heat stays on the actual supporting terrain; raised routes, cover and clear floor gaps provide safe alternatives.

Brief mechanical startups, visible phase damage and distinct wrecks bring the early bosses into the same presentation as the middle factory. The player can still move. Pause freezes the encounter, and the short destruction beat finishes before the native room-clear reward. No text overlays or extra health bars are added. Reduced Effects retains the full attack geometry, and offscreen warnings follow the Crane's physical hook or its separate flak muzzle as appropriate.
