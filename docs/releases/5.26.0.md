# 5.26.0 — Tools of the Trade

The three earned Toolroom guns now show their working mechanisms and respond with distinct procedural sound layers. Coil carbine coils discharge and recover around real shots; actual contacted targets receive brief local penetration marks. Twinbore cycles its paired barrels. Pressure repeater works its feed mechanism and builds a restrained pressure sound across actual sustained fire, with a short release when firing ends.

These effects follow real discharge cadence and the simulation clock. Pause freezes moving parts, Reduced Effects tones down motion, and native armor, cover and collision still decide every impact. Beam, shell, rail and mass conversions retain their ammunition artwork and sound. Weapon damage, firing rates, recoil, pellet counts and upgrade combinations are unchanged. No gameplay random stream is used for the new presentation.

## Try tool

On the Campaign weapon picker, select an **unlocked** gun and choose **Try tool**. The private range uses the real gun and standard movement, aiming and fire controls. Stationary targets make paired fire and penetration easy to inspect. Pause or reset freely; Back returns to the same selected gun without starting or replacing a run.

The range owns its own game and physics world. It awards no unlocks, discoveries or records and does not alter Continue, an active attempt, progress or saved settings. Locked weapons remain locked. A player's first Campaign still begins directly with the Service pistol without showing the picker.

This presentation update applies to existing runs as well as new ones. Campaign seed revisions, Daily rules, Practice records and save schemas are unchanged.
