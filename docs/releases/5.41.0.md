# 5.41.0 — Signal Machines

The Caller and Switchman gain working mechanical rigs, local damage reactions, health-based casing wear, distinct shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage.

## Working signal machinery

- **Caller:** a motorized horn, recording diaphragm and three-step mechanical queue follow actual sampled positions, committed marks and individual shots. Canceled recordings reset the mechanism.
- **Switchman:** grounded patrol feet, planted firing braces and a cycling receiver distinguish its own shots from the remote Annex port. Its antenna and radio follow actual charge, lock, admitted transmission and junction feedback.

The existing Caller ground marks, firing lanes and Annex junction/port warnings retain their rules. Rejected shots cannot invent recoil or a successful remote volley. Native AI, targeting, damage, timing, physical rounds and saved progress remain authoritative. Friendly blue identities, Pause and Reduced Effects are supported.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and reaction courses use the selected starting tool; shutdown and wreckage courses supply an upgraded tool. Wreckage courses use a real raised deck. A distant guard keeps the room active while shutdowns settle. These private courses preserve Continue, unlocks, discoveries and records.

## Counterplay and friendly comparisons

- [Caller recording](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=signal-machines&unit=caller&scene=mechanisms&v=1): move between its recorded positions, then leave those marks after lock. Push the braced hull out of its locked position to cancel the remaining queue.
- [Switchman junction feedback](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=signal-machines&unit=switchman&scene=mechanisms&v=1): shoot the amber junction while it charges. Native feedback interrupts the remote volley and damages its owner; it does not represent a successful transmission.
- Friendly comparisons: [Caller](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=signal-machines&unit=caller&scene=friendly&v=1), [Switchman](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=signal-machines&unit=switchman&scene=friendly&v=1). These private starting fixtures place a native blue ally against actual enemies. They do not grant a conversion, unlock or reward to your saved run. Player shots keep their ordinary friendly-fire rules.

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` changes the private starting tool. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes and unsupported link versions fail closed.
