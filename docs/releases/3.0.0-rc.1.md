# Recoil Foundry: Dead Signal — 3.0.0-rc.1

25 September 2026 · Local release candidate · Daily ruleset 84

**The shift ended. The orders didn't.**

A sealed communications wing has started issuing orders again. Take the new route after Furnace, follow the transmission, and turn the factory's own machines against it.

- **Transmission Annex:** an alternate four-room route within the twenty-room campaign, with six combat layouts, mirrored arrangements, and a new encounter at its heart.
- **Subversion:** five connected upgrades. Keep one stronger machine under your command, or build a short-lived group that overloads when its orders expire.
- **Two new machines:** interrupt a Switchman's remote transmission or move away from the positions a Caller records.
- **An original regional score:** “Orders After Hours,” with new combat layers and distinct charge, lock, interruption and reboot sounds.
- **New factory records:** original lore, discoveries, and victory-unlocked Practice.
- **Boss balance:** hovering under cover no longer silently cancels every aimed attack. When fixed mounts are blocked, the controller visibly takes over a firing lane. Its warning still locks before firing, cover still stops rounds, and the linked junction can still interrupt it.

New campaigns and Daily 84 use the revised encounter. Existing checkpoints and Daily 78–83 retain their saved rules, layouts and rewards. No progress reset is required.
