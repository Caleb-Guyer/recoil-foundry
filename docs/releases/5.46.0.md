# 5.46.0 — Material Response

Ordinary crates, movable cover, canisters and suspended cargo gain distinct fitted construction, accepted-hit reactions and health-based wear. Braces, panels, seams, caps and lifting hardware follow the actual material and body. Small contact details stay tied to real impacts, while flexible inner parts react inside the existing collision hull.

Actual destruction releases brief, bounded material fragments: splintered boards, bent plates, ruptured casing, freight panels, masonry chips and shelf struts. These fragments are harmless presentation. Native solid rubble remains physical, shootable and temporary, with its own clear collision outline. Removed wall and shelf hulls disappear when their native bodies break.

Registered weak walls and shelves gain anchor bolts, captive seams and clearer damage progression. Shelves retain a continuous visible landing plate above a riveted web. Accepted hits leave local chips; worn seams branch as actual health falls. Breaking the real shelf still releases supported loads through the existing native rules. Structural platforms, floors and protected machinery supports retain their role.

Canister hardware and steady brackets distinguish sealed casing, the short arming grace, armed state and an admitted impact. Shooting arms a canister with the existing 0.06-second grace before a qualifying impact, with no timed fuse. Direct heavy impacts and native chain reactions retain their immediate explosions, including sealed casing. Actual cover still occludes the physical explosion and chain response. Suspended cargo keeps its real cable, warning, release, falling body and landing rules.

Pause freezes the presentation in simulation time. Reduced Effects keeps construction, health wear, steady canister states and static terrain contact cues while suppressing transient movement. Native health, damage, collision hulls, platforms, knockback, arming, blast occlusion, support release, routes, rewards, audio, random streams and Continue retain their rules. Specialized job and hunt gear keeps its own artwork.
