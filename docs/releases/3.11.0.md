# Shifting Shafts — 3.11.0

Maintenance Shafts now change between runs. Each climb shuffles four hand-built sections, including all three section families and one repeat, into **36 possible arrangements per shaft**.

- **Piston shaft:** staggered press timings, paired heads that strike in sequence, and sheltered landings with small recoil perches.
- **Broken lift:** offset elevators, crumbling transfer plates, and twin lifts with opposite timing and a second climbing route.

Shoot downward to skip transfers or recover a missed landing. Crumbling plates return after the space clears. Every arrangement is reachable with the starting gun; enhanced recoil and Fold remain useful. The summit still awards one compatible upgrade without healing, and there are no new HUD panels or timers.

New campaigns receive the shuffled layouts. Continue preserves the exact arrangement; older saved shafts keep their original layout. Daily challenges and the chance of finding a shaft are unchanged.
