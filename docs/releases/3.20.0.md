# Recoil Foundry 3.20.0 — Fittings to earn

Completes the achievement and ability portion of [Variety and Longevity, issue #7](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/7). The preceding [3.19.0](3.19.0.md) supplies the collection grid, sightings, enemy and site records, and persistent per-entry notifications. [3.18.1](3.18.1.md) addresses [Faction War too easy, issue #8](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/8).

There are now **113 upgrades**. The original 108 keep their existing access. Five additions unlock through concrete achievements; the Logbook shows exact requirements and progress, plus a Next goals strip. Unlocking a fitting creates its own New notice. Encountering that fitting later recovers its effect and authored record, with a separate notice. Collecting it also adds it to Workshop. Opening one notice leaves the others unread.

| Fitting           | Achievement                                                                                          | Effect                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Double Jump       | Defeat the Loader or Crane. Existing recorded victories count.                                       | Press the usual jump control again for one airborne jump. Only landing restores it.                                                                  |
| Wing Harness      | Visit three different main zones. Existing visited zones count.                                      | Hold jump while descending to limit fall speed for up to 1.2 seconds per landing. Upward recoil remains intact.                                      |
| Ground Fault      | Defeat a boss with Arc Coil equipped.                                                                | An alternative Arc Coil branch: a 75%-strength floor pulse, up to three grounded targets within 190 units. It gives up ordinary aerial chaining.     |
| Static Reservoir  | Earn Air Traffic: six kills in one room without landing between kills. Existing commendations count. | Three airborne recoil shots store one cell. The next Arc discharge spends it on a half-strength bonus arc to an unvisited target. Landing drains it. |
| Conductive Tether | Link two hostile machines with Tether while Arc Coil is equipped.                                    | An alternative Cable branch requiring both parents. One live tether transfers 35% of the discharge damage to its other endpoint.                     |

Campaign and Daily runs can earn these achievements. Practice, Workshop and test presets cannot. Ground Fault competes with Daisy Chain and Short Circuit; Conductive Tether competes with Snapback and Grapnel. Both enter drafts from room-eight rewards onward, subject to parents and the unlock. Static Reservoir requires Arc Coil. Electrical effects respect cover, spawn grace, allies, directional shields and boss armor, and cannot recursively charge other arcs. Charge storage, glide time, target counts, transfers and drawn effects have fixed limits. The two movement additions use the existing keyboard, controller or touch jump control, with contextual Controls and Pause hints; they add no new binding.

## Runs and progress

A new Campaign snapshots the achievement fittings currently unlocked. Reward, reroll, special case and Reforge choices use that snapshot. Earning another fitting makes it eligible for the next Campaign; Continue keeps the current run's starting pool and pending choices. A run recap stores the same snapshot, and its replay link carries it. Older runs and recaps without that metadata retain their original pool. Published older versioned build-catalog links retain their previous guns.

Daily rulesets 78–85 retain their shared original pool and deterministic choices, independent of personal achievements. The five additions are Campaign draft rewards in this release. Daily can still contribute boss, exploration and weapon mastery achievements with its existing equipment.

The atomic progress profile includes a validated milestone category. Older profiles and backups migrate safely. Unlock evidence, collection states and unread acknowledgements survive reload, backup, restore and undo. Existing victories, exploration and Air Traffic backfill their corresponding goals; newly required Arc boss and linked-circuit evidence starts being recorded with this release.

## Validation

Type checking, the production build and all **2,227 tests** passed: **2,226 regression tests** plus an exhaustive Workshop physics check of all **9,216** complete legal builds. Each complete build fires through 180 simulated frames, with finite player/projectile physics, bounded secondary effects and cleanup on death. Automated checks also cover shared Daily pools, reward and Reforge eligibility, Continue and replay snapshots, profile migrations, per-entry unlock notices, backup/restore/undo, finite movement budgets, shield and cover interactions, and hostile-only achievement credit. The compatibility audit covers all **6,328** dependency-closed upgrade pairs in both acquisition orders. These complete stress builds exceed ordinary Campaign pick counts.

Ordinary-input playtests used live health, enemy attacks, recoil and physics. A mobility build cleared a Furnace room in 12.3 seconds with 100 health; an electrical/mobility build cleared a Cooling room in 18.45 seconds with 100 health. The eleven-fitting build defeated the Condenser in 64.97 seconds with 18 health using the campaign pilot's attack dodges. A simpler room pilot died to that boss, so the additions do not substitute for handling its attack pattern. These are reproducible automated playtests, not a claim of final player difficulty balance.

Production-browser checks covered locked progress, unlocked silhouettes, revealed records, single-entry acknowledgment across reload, Workshop fitting and compatibility, contextual controls, and responsive layout at 320 and 1280 pixels. The [final production pass](../production-pass-3.20.0.md) also verifies deployment and fixes misleading empty collection filters. Controller and touch use the existing input path; physical device testing and broader player balance feedback remain useful. The build still reports the existing large JavaScript chunk warning.
