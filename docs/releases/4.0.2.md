# Jobs begin in zone two — 4.0.2

New Campaign runs keep the opening Loading docks zone free of Uprising job offers and missions. The first choice appears after room 5, in the second zone, and its Railworks mission occupies room 6. Foundry Core moves to room 10; Reclamation and Rooftops remain in rooms 14 and 18. All four decisions and all eleven jobs stay available in the twenty-room campaign.

The campaign map, mission reservations and isolated previews follow the new schedule. Floodgate planning avoids reserved job rooms, so it cannot silently occupy the relocated Foundry Core mission slot.

Continue checkpoints and run recaps from earlier versions retain their original job schedule and recorded outcomes. New runs save the new schedule as Uprising run version 2; collection records and contract unlocks keep their existing format. Daily, Practice, Workshop and campaigns without Uprising keep their rules.

Validation passed: 160 focused regression tests, including all eleven mission-to-exit pilots, six complete campaigns, opening-zone rewards, old job checkpoints, mission reservations, Floodgate exclusions and profile recovery. The production build, TypeScript check, formatting and release-note generation also passed.
