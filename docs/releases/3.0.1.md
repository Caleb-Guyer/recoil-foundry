# Recoil Foundry 3.0.1 — A clearer way into the Annex

- **Choose your route at the door.** In new normal runs, the Cooling entrance no longer commits your route before you reach its visible threshold. After the Furnace fight, amber step lights and a small upward arrow make the Annex climb easier to notice without adding combat text.
- **Build for the route you chose.** Taking the Annex entrance offers an optional Subversion card before the first fight. Its first room also offers a legal follow-up when available. You still choose one of three cards; there are no extra upgrade slots, forced choices or unlocked opposing branches. Boss salvage remains available, and rerolls work normally.
- **Continue safely.** Existing checkpoints retain their reward rules and pending choices. Daily ruleset 84 remains unchanged, including its one fixed reward. These normal-run changes use Annex revision 6.

The post-launch technical audit reproduced the early door trigger and compared direct, Burst-beam and shell builds with both Subversion branches. Those checks did not justify a general weapon buff or boss nerf. Enemy attacks, weapon damage, ally limits and boss health are unchanged.
