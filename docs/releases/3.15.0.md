# Boss mastery — 3.15.0

Two new commendations reward learning the Loading Docks bosses:

- **Unsafe Load:** bait the Loader into ramming a standing cargo support, then defeat it. Earn the **Caution** hazard-striped gun finish and E. Holt’s incident report.
- **Clearance:** recoil-vault over the Crane’s sweeping head, hit it during that recovery, then defeat it. Earn the **Operator** outfit and T. Orr’s overhead-handling report.

Each requirement and its locked cosmetic appear after that boss has been defeated. Read objectives in the Logbook; a brief notice appears after earning one and clearing combat. Equip rewards in **Workshop → Appearance**. The character preview now stays visible while scrolling through desktop appearance choices.

Campaign and Daily victories count. Practice, Workshop and isolated tests cannot grant rewards. Recoil works with projectile and beam builds; portals, ordinary jumps, cancelled sweeps, unrelated recovery windows and automatic support collapses cannot stand in for the required moves. Restarting the room resets unfinished challenge evidence. Earned rewards and reports travel with progress backups.

Cosmetics do not change combat stats. Boss behavior, damage, warning times, upgrade generation, checkpoint format and Daily/Practice rules are unchanged.
