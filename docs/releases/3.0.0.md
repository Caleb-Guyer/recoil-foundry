# Recoil Foundry 3.0 — Dead Signal

**The shift ended. The orders didn’t.**

A sealed communications wing has started issuing orders again. After Furnace, take the upper exit into the **Transmission Annex**, or stay on the Cooling Works route.

- **A new four-room route.** Six combat layouts and mirrored arrangements bring broadcast floors, cable wells and receiver galleries into the twenty-room campaign.
- **Make the factory fight itself.** Five Subversion upgrades reboot defeated machines as temporary blue allies. Keep one stronger ally, or command a short-lived pair that can overload when its orders expire.
- **Two new threats.** Interrupt a Switchman’s remote transmission by shooting its charging junction. Move away from the positions a Caller records.
- **Follow the signal.** An original synth-rock theme, new factory records and an encounter best discovered inside the Annex.
- **A quiet introduction.** Optional “What’s new” notes on the title screen explain the update without adding combat clutter.

Still one gun. Now **105 upgrades**. Your saved progress stays intact. New runs use the latest encounter rules; existing checkpoints and Daily challenges retain their saved layouts, rewards and rules. Daily ruleset 84 includes the new content. Practice encounters still require an actual victory to unlock.
