# 5.34.0 — Machine Breakdown

Runners, gunners and flyers now have working chassis, reactions at the struck hull, distinct mechanical shutdowns and brief inactive wreckage. Their native health, hitboxes, attack timing, warnings, damage and rewards remain authoritative.

## Working machines

Runners plant and lift articulated feet, with exposed drives and braced attack poses. Gunners spread their stabilizers and cycle the receiver through native windup and recovery. Flyers bank around their existing hull on twin ducted rotors. Panel damage follows actual remaining health at the existing 65% and 30% thresholds.

The new chassis applies to standard, elite, mutated, squad, split-child and allied versions of these three families. Existing shield outlines, twin barrels, volatile warnings, mutation and squad markings, health displays and committed attack tells remain visible.

## Read the hit

A brief body-local contact distinguishes armor absorption from actual chassis damage using the native hit outcome. The reaction uses the supplied approach direction against the real hull; directionless damage does not invent a contact point. A cosmetic panel opening does not create a weak-point bonus.

## Take them apart

Runners buckle their legs, gunners fold their receiver and braces, and flyers lose rotor drive. The shutdown leaves a dark, inactive silhouette that settles toward a real supported surface, then fades. Moving support is tracked; removed support cannot leave a floating wreck. Wreckage has no physics body and cannot obstruct movement, shots or enemy decisions.

Presentation records are bounded: up to three contact marks per machine, 96 total contacts and 12 wrecks. Contacts last 0.24 seconds; shutdowns take 0.38 seconds and wrecks expire after 2.4 seconds. Room changes and returning to the title clear them. Pause freezes their game clock. Reduced Effects holds a restrained chassis and static defeated silhouette while retaining existing attack warnings; its wreck fades within 0.9 seconds. Unsupported airborne wrecks fade within 0.75 seconds rather than inventing a floor.

Bosses, cleanup/despawn kills, cinematic form changes and Workshop targets keep their existing presentation.
