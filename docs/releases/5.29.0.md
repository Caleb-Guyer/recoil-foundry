# 5.29.0 — Build Bench

Workshop makes the existing 161-fitting catalog easier to explore and test. Collected upgrades and unlocked guns keep their normal rules.

## Build with a reason

- Search fitting names and descriptions; combine mechanic filters with compatible, available-follow-up or fitted views. Filters inspect the complete current build and real path/prerequisite/fusion rules.
- Inspect a fitting before editing. Navigate collected prerequisites and follow-ups, read specific conflict explanations, and review the complete cascade before confirming a removal. Clear build is reviewed too. An edit can be undone while reviewing its comparison.
- Compare the actual full gun before and after fitting or removing an upgrade, including every dependent removed with it. The same native simulation used by reward comparisons powers the pictures; it does not invent an aggregate DPS number. Pausing or leaving the editor stops the preview, and disposal removes its temporary physics world.
- The fitted-build overview stays behind one expandable control. Appearance, saved blueprints, weapon locks and limited boss Practice builds retain their roles. Normal Workshop never supplies uncollected fittings.

## Choose the range

Choose a scenario in the editor or from the compact range selector during play. Changing scenario or resetting reconstructs real targets, props, destructible terrain, ammunition and fitting cooldowns while preserving the selected gun and build.

| Scenario        | What to test                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grouped targets | Real penetration, fragments, chain effects and area damage across close and elevated target groups.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Incoming fire   | Fully warned, committed hostile rounds exercise actual reflection, armor, dodge and recovery fittings. The existing health meter is visible. A supply crate behind the entrance can earn Scrap Armor through its normal trigger. Native defeat resets this private range at full health with the same gun, fittings and scenario. |
| Cover & banks   | Bank rounds around steel, break real props and damaged terrain, then reset to restore the room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Recoil course   | Climb supported landing decks with ordinary jumps and your gun’s actual recoil; exercise movement fittings and recover a missed launch.                                                                                                                                                                                           |

The original Open range and first-session warm-up remain available with their established geometry and safe targets. Range simulations grant no Campaign rewards, discoveries, weapon licenses, boss victories or records, and cannot replace Continue.
