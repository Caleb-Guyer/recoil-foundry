# Rupture — 5.13.0

The Reclaimer is now a transforming finale with three distinct forms, a changing physical arena, an original layered score and a shutdown sequence. Fresh Campaigns use this fight in room twenty. The Interceptor remains the earlier security supervisor; the Reclaimer is the machine that copies the player's gun.

## Three forms

| Form             | Encounter                                                                                                                         | Arena and response                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mimic**        | The compact rival opens with aimed fire and a heavy volley, then draws from its weapon arsenal.                                   | Learn its locked aim and punish the exposed armor between attacks. A brief entrance can be skipped with Jump or a fresh fire press.                                                                                                      |
| **Siege frame**  | At 68% health, weapon pods unfold and the Reclaimer begins linking pairs of attacks.                                              | The room rebuilds into a rising series of siege decks. Marked floor batteries strike sections of the lower arena. Move off the marked section or climb above it.                                                                         |
| **Rupture core** | At 34% health, the armor opens into a suspended reactor with additional weapon arms. Three attacks link before a longer recovery. | The roof spars tear apart and the decks change again. Reactor columns alternate with horizontal crosscuts. One column marks your position, then locks; leave it, use the wide gaps, change height, and attack during the longer opening. |

Each form has a health gate so a strong burst cannot skip a transformation. Damage is paused during the entrance and the two 2.5-second transformations. Active rounds, pending volleys and old field attacks are cleared. Terrain changes halfway through the paused transformation; any intersecting player hull is placed on top of the new support before play resumes. The base floor remains a safe physical fallback between marked strikes. The boss HUD shows the form and the two health thresholds.

Normal health increases by 20% over 5.12.0. New Game+ increases by 18% over its previous health, starts with all nineteen weapon patterns, links paired attacks from the first form, broadens floor and reactor strikes, narrows the crosscut's center gap, and gives shorter rests between arena strikes. Weapon warnings retain their existing timing. New arena strikes always warn for **1.65 seconds** and damage once when released; a delayed frame cannot skip the warning.

Sabotage disables the arena batteries and reactor strikes along with its existing weapon restrictions. Crew help retains its single volleys and regular recovery, and restores its two barricades on the rebuilt floor. Overloaded production unlocks advanced weapons sooner. Cargo pursuit keeps the same three-form Reclaimer. No extra patrol is stacked into the transformed boss arena.

## Presentation and payoff

The entrance establishes the rival before control returns. Armor pods unfold and the final core has its own silhouette. A new original 128-step theme adds a driven layer in Siege frame and a higher lead and pulse in Rupture core without restarting the phrase. New Game+ uses a faster arrangement. Aimed, fan and field warnings have different sound cues; reserved warning voices and music ducking keep them audible under gunfire.

Gun warnings, field marks and arena boundaries render above friendly effects. Reduced effects keeps those warnings, the form silhouettes and the phase information while suppressing camera movement and extra flash accents. The three-second shutdown dismantles the machine, cancels attacks and holds room clearance until it finishes. Campaign extraction and the existing pictured reward cards follow; ordinary Campaign results acknowledge the three-form victory, and Overtime has its own acknowledgment.
