# Room to Move — 4.4.0

Fresh Campaigns now alternate combat rhythms. Ambushes open with rushing machines and bring support in quickly; crossfire rooms introduce ranged pressure in smaller groups; elevated fights reward climbing to the authored firing positions. Room one opens with a single basic patrol machine. Ordinary rooms immediately after bosses use slower arrivals and a lower live enemy cap. Seeds vary the rhythm within each zone; existing layouts, enemy rosters and physical spawn anchors remain intact. Special enemy introductions and Factory events retain their own opening fights.

Reinforcements arrive in staggered pairs, while coordinated formations keep their members in the same wave. Doors retain their full warning before opening and wait when the live enemy cap is full. Ordinary gun warnings avoid stacking the same lane or an opposing pincer, and chargers, hoppers and snipers reserve a movement opening before starting their tell. Visible warnings, committed attacks, twin follow-ups and rounds already in flight keep their timing. Squads and special machines retain their authored attack patterns.

Jobs use pacing appropriate to the objective: ambushes for recovery and sabotage, crossfire for defense, and traversal for evacuation. Defense generators activate only after the patrol is clear, with a matching status hint, then hold for the full eighteen seconds. Defense arrivals have staggered firing delays, and attacks aimed at the generator share the lane schedule even through destructible cover. Skipping a job requires moving right on the ground through the exit after the patrol settles; recoil alone cannot abandon it.

Boss recovery lasts at least one current firing cycle plus a short reaction allowance, bounded between 0.6 and 1.05 seconds; longer authored recoveries stay longer. This gives the pistol, shotgun and nailgun time to use an exposed opening without changing their damage, recoil or unlocks.

Continue saves and Recent runs retain their encounter revision. Older saved runs and their replays retain the prior schedule. Timed special rooms such as Floodgate, story encounters, courier rooms and the Sorting Pit keep their authored schedules. Daily challenges, Practice, Workshop, Overtime and optional detours keep their existing rules. Pause and hitstop freeze pending arrivals and attack admission; room changes clear waiting attackers. No new combat HUD panels are added.

Isolated previews use real controls and rendering without recording progress: `?test=encounters&room=1&gun=pistol`, `?test=encounters&room=3&gun=shotgun`, `?test=encounters&room=4&gun=nailgun` and `?test=encounters&route=core-defense&gun=nailgun`. Rooms 1–20 and all eleven job route IDs are supported.
