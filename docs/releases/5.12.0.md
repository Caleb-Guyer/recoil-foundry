# Final Arsenal — 5.12.0

The player-mimicking rival is now **the Reclaimer**. The other mobile security boss is **the Interceptor**. Names follow their actual machines throughout the Logbook, Practice, damage messages, arena descriptions and records; discoveries and saved victories stay attached to the same machines.

Fresh Campaigns meet the Sorter or Interceptor in Reclamation, then face the Reclaimer in room 20. Every job outcome keeps that final opponent. New Game+ returns to the Reclaimer in its storm arena.

## Nineteen weapon attacks

The Reclaimer's existing sixteen weapon tricks now have room to appear in the remixed final arenas. Skybridge and Crossfire no longer limit new fights to a four-attack loop. Three new patterns bring its weapon arsenal to nineteen, alongside physical recoil vaults:

- **Sweepfire:** three successive five-shot fans sweep across different locked angles. Each volley has its own warning; the fourth New Game+ volley returns through the center.
- **Ringburst:** radial volleys leave three wide gaps. The next warned release rotates those gaps. New Game+ adds a third release.
- **Lockdown:** three fixed circles mark real floor or platform surfaces before small charges arm there. They do not chase the player after appearing. New Game+ marks four positions.

Ricochets, burst fire, precision rounds, scatter fire, fuses, returning rounds, afterimages, countershots, capacitor shots, shattering rounds, portals, landing shockwaves and surface saws retain their own effects and warnings.

The opening phase has eight eligible weapon patterns; phase two expands to sixteen and the last phase opens all nineteen. A completed attack is removed from the current rotation until the remaining eligible weapons have appeared. Changing phase introduces unused weapons rather than repeating the two-attack introduction. Layout and seed vary the order deterministically.

Later phases link two attacks with a shorter recovery between them, then expose the armor for a longer punish window. Queued bursts, echoes, charges and surface saws finish before the next attack can begin. Phase changes cancel owned effects and give a one-second transition. Shot limits, locked aim, physical cover and Reduced effects remain in force. Lit armor vents indicate the current phase.

Job consequences still matter: sabotage disables the ring and field attacks, crew help suppresses follow-up volleys, and overloaded production unlocks a broader selection earlier. Cargo pursuit retains the same Reclaimer rather than substituting another machine.

## Harder New Game+

The return fight starts with all nineteen weapons available. It links attacks more tightly, adds Sweepfire and Ringburst follow-ups, expands Lockdown and slightly increases ammunition damage. Its existing Overtime health scaling, lightning lanes and staged supports remain, with additional durability for the revised boss. Marked warnings are never sped up.
