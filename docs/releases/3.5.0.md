# Stormfront — 3.5.0

Overtime Rooftops now has five authored layouts:

- **Antenna approach:** split firing positions, a partly exposed lightning lane and a sheltered upper walkway.
- **Lightning masts:** staggered high platforms and open ascent corridors.
- **Sheltered maintenance:** a lower route beneath long protective canopies.
- **Exposed skyline:** an upper route with three conductor lanes across elevated firing positions.
- **Storm relay:** the Interceptor finale, with alternating lightning lanes and short shelter platforms.

Fixed conductors give a full 1.5-second visual and electrical warning before lightning strikes. Only one lane activates at a time. Move an enemy into its path, then use recoil to escape. Strikes hurt the player and exposed enemies; bosses retain their armor and attacks. Roofs and physical cover block lightning. Removing or moving cover during a warning cannot extend that strike into an unannounced area. The next warning can expose it again.

Rain stays in the background, lightning stays inside its marked lane, and Reduced effects retains static warning geometry without rain or jagged bolts. There are no new HUD panels or controls. Lightning stops when combat ends. Permanent ground and low obstacles keep every route traversable with ordinary jumps.

New Overtime entries use revision 5, completing the authored remixes across all five areas. Existing saves keep their original generation. Campaign, Daily, upgrades and earned ordinary boss Practice retain their rules. Practice ruleset remains 1. Rollbacks must preserve revision-5 checkpoint decoding and generation.
