# Recoil Foundry 3.0.5 — Beat your best

- **Practice records.** Wins save your fastest time, fewest hits, and the exact gun for each boss arena. The boss setup shows a small preset best; **Records** opens the saved builds.
- **Challenge a friend.** Copy a challenge after a win or from a saved record. **Practice → Import challenge** previews the same boss arena, gun and target time. Retry keeps that challenge intact.
- **Earned access.** Players must have defeated the boss and collected every upgrade in the challenge. Locked bosses and undiscovered upgrades remain unnamed. Challenge guns stay fixed during the attempt.
- Records keep separate balance versions. Older versions remain archived and cannot compete against current rules. Timing excludes pauses; hits count damage that actually gets through, even if you later heal.
- Practice records are included in progress backups. Older profiles and backups load with an empty record list. Campaign saves, Daily rules, combat balance and unlock requirements are unchanged.

Records are personal to this browser, with up to 200 saved arena/build combinations. Share codes carry a target for friendly competition, not a verified online leaderboard. Practice still awards no campaign progress, discoveries, boss unlocks or cosmetic commendations.
