# 5.32.0 — Living Foundry

The five ordinary factory zones now have authored scenery, connected working machinery and playable, caption-free entry reveals. Campaign and Quick Shift share the new visual pass; combat and generation revisions are unchanged.

## Five districts, fifteen compositions

- **Loading docks:** cargo-transfer gantry, drydock vessel and cargo-elevator hall. Rail-mounted hoists, pulleys and suspended loads establish the factory's scale.
- **Furnace halls:** blast tower, casting cathedral and rolling trench. Charging conveyors, hanging ladles, connected flywheels and rollers work around the furnace vessels.
- **Cooling Works:** paired cooling towers, vaulted circulation hall and raised aqueduct reservoir. Pumps, fan drives and circulation marks follow the cooling service.
- **Reclamation Works:** traveling magnet gantry, shear press and tilted sorting drum. Scrap lines and processing machinery use the salvage circuit.
- **Rooftops:** service tower, ventilation plant and suspended service bridge. A layered city skyline, air-handling drives and wind details extend the factory into the distance.

Each room gets a reproducible composition without consuming the gameplay random stream. Shallow parallax, restrained light and quiet silhouettes keep backgrounds distinct from the pale edges of real platforms and cover.

## Walk into the view

On first entry into a district, architectural gates, heat shields and observation-hangar leaves open as you explore the entry. The doors stay behind the game world. Keep moving, jumping, aiming and firing throughout: there is no caption, cinematic input lock, automatic camera pan or full-screen wipe. Backtracking does not close the reveal again.

Later rooms in the same district start open. Quick Shift follows the actual first occurrence of each district in its five-room plan. Its compressed stage numbers cannot trigger the wrong zone reveal. Existing mid-district Continue saves open ordinary scenery directly; no new save fields are required.

## Real machine states

Motor phase advances only with active simulation and live power. Pause, results and upgrade menus stop it. Native blackouts and a disconnected room's circuit extinguish local task lights and stop its motors. Restoring the real fuse restores the machinery without catching up missed animation time.

Successful Core sabotage disables the affected cooling and ventilation services while independent lighting, conveyors and salvage machinery remain supplied. Clearing a patrol alone does not switch the factory off. This is visual scenery: no extra hazards, collision, forces, sounds or rewards are introduced.

Specialized maintenance courses, Annex, freight shafts, story rooms, shutdown staging and the Reclaimer finale retain their authored scenery. The final-approach skyline staging and its aircraft remain above the ordinary background layer.

## Readability and rendering

Reduced Effects uses static machine poses while preserving architecture, exploration state and circuit indicators. Foreground platforms, characters and attack warnings keep their normal resolution and rendering order.

The background uses one reusable raster capped at **960 × 600 pixels**, independent of the foreground's display density. Unchanged paused or Reduced Effects views reuse its paint; camera movement, exploration and actual service changes refresh it. Geometry loops, gradients and canvas state are regression-checked across all fifteen compositions, mirrors and wide viewports.

`scripts/living-foundry-audit.ts` generates real-renderer contact sheets and before/after native Canvas CPU measurements at 1280 × 720 and 2560 × 1440. These measurements describe the local CPU raster pass, not browser GPU frame rate or a human playtest.
