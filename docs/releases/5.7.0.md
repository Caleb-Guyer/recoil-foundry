# Hunting Grounds — 5.7.0

Boss hunts are now a predictable route to the Coil carbine. New Campaigns offer **two different optional hunts**, in **room 9 (zone 3)** and **room 17 (zone 5)**. The pair favors machines you have not beaten. Clear the patrol, then jump at the marked boss-hunt door; taking the ordinary exit skips that hunt without cancelling the later one.

## Three purpose-built arenas

- **Cable vault:** staggered cable platforms give elevated routes over live floor cables; shoot the anchors to cut their power.
- **Plate depot:** elevated firing angles let you reach the Bulwark above its movable shields, with an open floor lane for pushing or breaking them.
- **Demolition bay:** spaced refuges give room to dodge marked charges. The Demolisher opens with a warned volley before alternating with explosives.

Each hunt begins with a quiet approach corridor. Crossing the lit threshold spawns the boss and closes a solid gate behind you. Winning opens the gate and offers a repair or a free upgrade reroll, then returns you to the cleared patrol with your build intact. Free rerolls remain available across rooms, and two earned credits can be spent on separate upgrade choices.

Entrances are reserved alongside factory encounters and jobs. They also work on the Transmission Annex route, with a clear landing and no overlapping props. Doors are visible before clearance and explain when they become usable.

## Saves and progression

Continue preserves both hunts and whether you are approaching, fighting, or choosing a reward. Retry and Recent Runs replays preserve the original pair. Existing Continue saves retain the old one-hunt schedule. Previously earned hunt victories, cosmetics, Practice access and Coil carbine licenses remain valid.

Practice records now use ruleset 3 because the hunt arenas changed. Earlier records remain readable, with their earlier ruleset, and do not compete against the redesigned arenas. Daily generation remains unchanged.
