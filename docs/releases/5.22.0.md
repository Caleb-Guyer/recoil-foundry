# 5.22.0 — Boss Remaster

The Condenser and Sorter now have three distinct attack phases and authored arenas in new Campaigns.

The Condenser commits to pressure volleys, leaves four passages through its purge ring, then introduces cooling fronts along marked floor lanes. Later phases link separately warned purge and pressure attacks and threaten two floor lanes together. Raised decks remain above the cooling fronts. Coolant strips reroute between phases; their existing slippery-floor behavior is preserved.

The Sorter begins with locked induction strikes and scrap fans, then adds a low magnetic sweep followed by separately warned blades. Its last phase links induction and scrap pressure. Floor belts stop during phase breaks, reverse for phase two, and accelerate inward for phase three. Magnetic lanes change from one flank to the other and then both; held cargo completes its native cycle before reconfiguration, and newly activated magnets always give their full warning.

Both machines have brief visual startups, mechanical phase breaks and destruction sequences. The player can still move. Existing health bars, native health, recovery armor, damage and salvage rules remain authoritative; attack warnings stay above friendly effects and retain their geometry with Reduced Effects.

## Compatibility

Start a **new Campaign** for the remaster (Campaign revision 102). Earlier Continue, Daily, New Game+ and recorded Practice encounters retain their arenas and attack rules. Explicit earned remix Practice still opens its original remix. Newly earned standard boss Practice and private room rematches reproduce the revised encounter. Job outcomes that remove coolant keep it removed.

Ordinary-input simulations exercise complete fights and traversal in both mirrors; these are technical checks rather than human playtest or win-rate claims.
