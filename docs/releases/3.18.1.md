# Recoil Foundry 3.18.1 — Faction pressure

Addresses the Campaign balance report in [issue #8](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/8).

Fresh Factory Shift conflicts now field six red enemies against three blue allies in the Loading Docks, and ten against five in the Furnace. Ground units use supported, separated positions; layouts without enough ground positions fill their roster with safely placed flyers. Later conflicts retain their authored ground, crossfire, or air formation and reserve six additional red flyers behind visible reinforcement doors. The existing warning, occupancy checks and active enemy cap govern their entry. Allies stay in combat and the cache stays locked until the reserves have arrived and been defeated.

Factory balance revision 2 is saved with the run and its recap. Continue and explicit `fv=1` replays retain the 3.18.0 formations and counts. The Factory condition and encounter schedule use the original seed stream. Historical Daily encounters retain their existing rules.

Validation covers complete opening rosters across seeded layouts, the reported `NRW1FY` encounter, inactivity versus ordinary player inputs, warning time and active enemy limits, old checkpoint and recap compatibility, cache eligibility, and campaign completion with three factory conditions. These simulations establish representative playable paths; human playtesting should inform further difficulty tuning.
