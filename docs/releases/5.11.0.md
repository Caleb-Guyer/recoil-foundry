# Enemy Remaster — 5.11.0

Three familiar patrol machines gain more deliberate behavior in fresh Campaigns. Their health, ammunition damage and projectile counts retain their existing rules. Room one keeps its original simple patrol, with no new encounter or behavior.

| Machine | Introduction  | What changes                                                                                                                                      | How to respond                                                                                                        |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Runner  | Room 3 onward | Braces for 0.5 seconds, commits to a short straight rush, then recovers for 0.65 seconds. Checks real walls and ledges before advancing.          | Jump across the marked rush, then attack during recovery. Bracing and recovering runners do not deal contact damage.  |
| Shooter | Zone 2 onward | Walks a short distance along its current solid platform to find a useful firing lane, plants its braces and locks one shot for 0.55 seconds.      | Change height or move behind solid cover after the aim locks. It cannot track you during the warning.                 |
| Flyer   | Zone 3 onward | Approaches from alternating flanks between volleys, stops and locks all three firing lanes for 0.7 seconds. Checks physical scenery while moving. | Leave the spread by changing height. Its next approach changes sides after the shot, with no mid-warning retargeting. |

Ranged machines pause after firing before repositioning. Shooters stay on their own platform, avoid occupied destinations and brake when support is missing. Moving or unsupported gunner perches retain their original behavior. The existing encounter scheduler admits new attacks before their visible warning; committed tells retain their full duration. Freeze, pause and hitstop suspend the same simulation clocks. Rebooted allies leave the hostile patrol state machine.

## Three authored encounters

Eligible fresh Campaign rooms can become **Pursuit lane** (room 3), **Split-level patrol** (room 9), or **Mortar crossing** (room 17). Each opens with one machine and holds backup until that opening is cleared or ten combat seconds have passed. Later entries keep their existing door warnings and a maximum of three live patrol members. Authored jobs, hunts, special introductions, Factory events, alternate routes and Security layouts take priority over these room replacements.

- **Pursuit lane:** a runner patrols the ground beneath a gunner's broad upper deck. Low steps support a jump or recoil approach to the deck.
- **Split-level patrol:** a repositioning gunner holds the central deck, with reachable launch steps on both sides. A later runner and flyer encourage changing height.
- **Mortar crossing:** a flanking flyer opens the fight before a mortar and runner arrive. Solid overhead shelters and upright cover interrupt firing lanes and shells; the ground route remains open.

The three rooms mirror with their actors, props and traversal routes. Every ground route works forward and backward with ordinary jumps, and the Split-level gunner deck is reachable with all six unmodified starting tools from either mirrored entrance.

## Read the chassis

The three base machines have extra chassis details: runner drive links, gunner stabilizers and flyer motor housings. Below 65% and 30% health, cracked panels, dimmed idle lamps and exposed mechanisms show wear. These are cosmetic and never slow a damaged machine or introduce a new damage multiplier. Ordinary legacy patrols share the visual detailing while retaining their original behavior.

Revised patrol deaths replace part of the generic spark burst with four short-lived chassis fragments. Existing particle limits, threat readability and Reduced effects apply. Attack warnings remain on the machine and its actual firing lanes; no extra combat panel is added. Logbook machine guides describe both the fresh Campaign behavior and older rules.

## Saves and validation

Campaign revision **93** owns the revised behavior and room selection. Continue, room retries, Recent Runs seed replay and death-room rematches reconstruct that revision from the saved seed. Earlier Campaign seeds, existing Continue saves, Daily rules 90 and older supported Dailies retain their behavior. Bosses, elites, mutations, coordinated squads, special encounters, Workshop and boss Practice keep their own AI. Upgrade pools, weapon licenses and save formats are unchanged.

Eighteen focused regression checks cover locked aim and minimum warnings, actual projectile directions, ledges, cover, support removal, solo waves, mirrored hull clearance, unmodified traversal with every tool, first-room exclusions, pause/hitstop, ally conversion, Continue and repeated rematch disposal, strict preset URLs and zero checkpoint writes. The regular regression suite and all eight maximal-build CI shards remain required for deployment.

`node --experimental-strip-types scripts/enemy-remaster-audit.ts output.json` runs 36 deterministic ordinary-input attempts across the three encounters, six native tools and both mirrors. The scripted controller cleared 35, with no deaths and one 60-second timeout underneath the Split-level gunner deck. Separate physical traversal checks reach that deck with every unmodified tool. This is simulation coverage, not a human balance rating or an observed playtest; it does not establish that all players will find the encounters fair.
