# Build Momentum — 5.1.0

New Campaigns give developing builds more continuity. Dense fights keep enemy warnings visible, and beam rendering spends less work on redundant glow and distant geometry.

## Upgrade follow-ups

Ordinary reward drafts after rooms **6, 9, 12, 15 and 18** reserve the last choice for an eligible upgrade with prerequisites you already own, if none of the three ordinary draws already supplies one. The first two draws stay open. Eligible follow-ups respect earned fitting licenses, fusion gates, mutually exclusive branches and reroll exclusions.

Actual follow-ups carry a small **Build follow-up** tag beside the existing path label. Hover or focus the card to compare the combined gun using the existing preview. This adds no permanent HUD panel.

Boss salvage and special authored introductions retain priority. The Annex can introduce Subversion instead of reserving that slot; the new rule never invents a follow-up when the eligible pool is empty or was excluded by a reroll. Daily still gives one card, and Overtime keeps its existing draft system.

Campaign revision **90** owns the new drafts. Revision 89 and earlier seeds retain their published offers. Continue preserves a pending offer and paid rerolls; Recent Runs can replay revision 88, 89 and 90 records.

## Clearer, cheaper combat

- Mortar paths, landing warnings and shootable shells render above friendly beams and cosmetic effects.
- Broad beam halos are grouped into at most two strokes. Crossings no longer stack bright fog. Every visible beam core remains; offscreen rays are omitted from drawing only.
- Nearby cosmetic impact sparks share one effect. Reduced effects removes the broad halo while retaining cores and warnings.
- Swept collision queries reject geometry outside the swept hull before doing the existing convex collision calculation. Mortar prediction builds its solid-body list once per prediction. Moving and rotated cover still uses its actual current geometry.
- The diagnostic page covers eleven scenarios, including heat beams and chain blasts against twin mortars. Select one case for a thirty-second test or two-minute soak; all patterns still take 5.5 or 22 minutes. Duration, environment and pause/resume labels match the selected test.

The six starting tools retain their damage, cadence and recoil. The before/after ordinary-input audit recorded identical combat outcomes for its shared strategy while improving scheduled follow-up coverage from 13/37 to 37/37 drafts. Deaths and navigation timeouts remain visible in the [validation report](../validation/build-momentum-5.1.0.md); these bots do not establish human win rates or complete Overtime balance.
