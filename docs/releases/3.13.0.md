# The Sorting Pit — 3.13.0

A rare Reclamation combat room puts a broad scrap magnet overhead. It lifts loose crates and debris out of the fight, removing cover, then releases the load after a warning hum and landing shadows. Falling scrap damages enemies and the player.

- Shoot the exposed green coil during lifting to release the load early. Even manual releases retain a short warning; repeated fire cannot postpone or skip it.
- Three seeded layouts: sheltered decks, inward conveyor feeds, and a recessed sorting floor with raised loading bays. Each can be mirrored. All have permanent escape routes and traversable floors.
- The magnet affects loose props, leaving player movement, enemies and projectiles unaffected. Lifted props retain real collisions. Settled scrap becomes ordinary cover again.
- The finite load can be destroyed. No replacement crates or bonus reward farming. Machinery powers down after combat, and the usual room upgrade follows.
- No new HUD, tutorial panel or timer. Moving cover, opening coil shutters, sound and floor markings communicate the cycle. Reduced effects retains warning marks.

Fresh normal campaigns have a 40% encounter roll for room 15 when it does not conflict with an area event or Auditor visit. Reclamation's introductions stay intact. Continue preserves the plan; earlier saves and Daily identities keep their previous rooms. Overtime, Practice and the Annex remain unchanged.
