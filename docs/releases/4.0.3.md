# Power and projectile lighting — 4.0.3

The first room always begins with an ordinary fight. Blackout, Turf War and Freight surge no longer replace its layout or patrol, including when continuing an older Factory plan. New Uprising campaigns introduce their Factory condition in room two and return to it in room five; job offers still begin in zone two.

Blackout fuse boxes are solid, floor-mounted cabinets. Players and enemies collide with the housing before and after activation. Placement keeps the cabinet clear of terrain, patrol spawns, coolant and moving machinery, with an accessible approach. Player fire disables the live circuit and restores the room's lights. The cabinet stays in place with an unlit indicator and switched-off mechanism, and releases the reserve only once. Continue restores the same inert housing after a completed outage.

Projectiles illuminate nearby scenery with a glow matching their ammunition. Ordinary rounds cast a small warm light; ice and electrical shots use cool colors, while shells, molten rounds and charged energy have larger pools. Fragments, echoes and parked rounds emit less light. Beams illuminate their actual traced path, and arcs light their endpoints. Hostile ammunition retains its own colors. Lights reveal the room during a blackout and add a subtle glow in lit rooms; offscreen culling and a fixed light budget bound busy volleys.

Verification covers first-room protection for new and legacy campaigns, physical player/enemy collisions, actual gun and beam activation, persistent housings, seeded placement, save restoration, ammunition lighting and complete campaign navigation. Browser checks confirm the live/inert cabinet and projectile glow.
