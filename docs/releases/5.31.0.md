# 5.31.0 — Quick Shift

Choose **Play → Quick Shift** for a complete five-room gun-building run. Campaign remains the twenty-room mode, and each mode keeps its own Continue slot.

## Five rooms, faster builds

Start with three sequential fitting choices. After each of the first four room clears, choose two more upgrades. Every choice previews your complete current gun before and after the fitting; the eleven picks preserve native compatibility, prerequisites, weapon patterns and morph/fusion requirements.

A seeded opening patrol leads to two distinct tactical encounters, a fourth-room optional job and one remastered Loader, Crane, Press or Kiln fight. Rooms use actual terrain, cover, machinery, reinforcement warnings and collision. The final boss keeps its native phases and arena transformations. Clear each ordinary patrol and take its right exit; the final boss ends the shift when defeated.

Choose a low or high route after the first room. Before room four, accept or skip a seeded clean-clear or 45-second rush job. Success repairs up to 15 health; failure has no additional penalty. A single objective cue shows the active condition during that room. Rush time is active room time at the combat clear; walking to the exit after clearing does not fail it.

## Replayable seeds

Open **Replay a seed** in setup to enter a short code or the complete `RF-Q1-` seed. Copy the seed or a challenge link from the result screen, replay it immediately, or choose a new one. The same version, seed, starting gun and sequence of choices reproduce the room plan and upgrade offers. Different draft choices can lead to different later offers.

Quick Shift uses a fixed standard pool so a shared challenge cannot silently change with another player's collection. Campaign-licensed fittings remain outside this pool. Earned starting guns remain gated by the existing weapon unlocks. A player's first run does not open a weapon picker; isolated gun fixtures are explicitly labelled as tests.

Results show completed rooms, active time, hits, shots, final fittings and the optional-job outcome. Best clears are compared only within the same seed, gun, route and job choice. Failed attempts cannot become clear records. Saved records are bounded and separate from Recent Campaign Runs, Daily, Gauntlet, Security and Overtime progress.

## Keep both runs

Quick Shift's separate Continue preserves sequential draft choices, route and job decisions, the exact next seeded offers and local measured combat totals. Combat resumes from that room's entry with its original enemies and terrain; already spent active time, shots and job-hit penalties remain counted. Pause does not advance the clock.

Both Continue slots and Quick Shift records are included in the existing atomic progress backup, import and undo workflow. Older profiles migrate without losing their Campaign, unlocks or records. Malformed Quick Shift data cannot partially replace a restored profile. No Quick Shift victory grants a Campaign clear, weapon license, cosmetic, discovery, Security level or New Game+ unlock.

## Validation

Native regressions cover deterministic room plans and acquisition sequences, current weapon/pool gates, all four bosses' phases and machinery, native movement and firing, five-room victory, clean/rush jobs, real damage and activation counters, Continue and backup isolation, corrupt-input rejection and private test protection. Browser checks use Sound and Music off.

Campaign generation, balance and recorded Daily identities retain their current revisions. Quick Shift has its own pinned ruleset and seed namespace. This update does not claim a measured human completion time; real player sessions are needed to establish typical duration and difficulty.
