# Room rematches & replay guidance — 5.9.0

After dying in a combat room, choose **Practice this room** from the results or replay. The rematch opens paused with full health and the gun and cosmetics from your finished run. The authored room, enemies, cover, physical props and room machinery start fresh. **Retry room** resets that practice attempt immediately. **Back to run results** returns to your original result; its replay remains available.

Practice runs in a separate temporary game. It grants no rewards, unlocks, saved records or Campaign advancement, and does not overwrite Continue, Daily results or the original run summary. Clearing the room ends practice there. **Again** on the original result still starts a new run. Existing earned boss Practice, Workshop, movement trials, extraction and the scripted shutdown sequence keep their existing retry flows; already-cleared return rooms do not offer a combat rematch.

**Watch replay** now includes one short suggestion based on the recorded lethal damage category and enemy when known. Mortar shells, sniper shots, collisions, falling cargo, environmental hazards and falls have different guidance. Unavailable causes receive a general review suggestion without an invented attacker or missed dodge. The same tip sits in a closed disclosure beside the rematch. Replay export retains its existing source marker; the extra tip appears in the viewer.

The rematch uses your normal keyboard bindings, mouse aim/fire and controller controls. Pause or leaving the active tab freezes practice. Back returns to results; the normal retry binding retries only the practice room while it is open.
