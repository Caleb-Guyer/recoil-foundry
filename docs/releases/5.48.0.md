# 5.48.0 — Unconventional Arsenal

Three earned starting weapons add new attack and movement decisions. Each has
its own weapon model, procedural sound, controls and live status display.

| Tool           | How it works                                                                                                                                                                                        | Unlock                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Bellows Cannon | Hold fire to inhale, tug yourself forward and pull exposed light machines or loose scrap closer. Release a short shrapnel volley and kick backward. Scrap improves the blast but is never required. | Defeat the Press or Kiln in Campaign or Daily. |
| Debt Collector | Bullets fire immediately and bank their actual recoil. Release to vent opposite the current aim; a full chamber automatically vents.                                                                | Complete a Campaign.                           |
| Switchjack     | Fire a slow live bolt. A second press takes its place and consumes its remaining damage.                                                                                                            | Win two different Campaign miniboss hunts.     |

Switchjack requires a clear route for the entire player hull. Cover, occupied
space, world boundaries and crusher sweeps block relocation. A bolt that has
hit, banked, expired or parked cannot be used. Jumping grants no invulnerability
or free portal bonuses. Recoil and momentum remain physical.

Bellows cannot consume mission objects or intact crates. Heavy machines resist
its intake, and cover blocks suction. Charge, scrap and recoil debt are bounded.
Pause, death, room changes and fresh runs clear transient mechanisms; resuming
with fire held cannot secretly discharge stored pressure or jump to a stale bolt.
Quick Bellows and Switchjack taps survive the brief impact freeze and are consumed
once; pausing or resetting cancels them.

Ordinary upgrades keep their real damage, patterns and payloads. Cutting Torch
explicitly converts Bellows and Switchjack to a beam, replacing their intake or
live-bolt trigger. Seeker Rack likewise replaces those triggers with target
painting and a released salvo. Debt Collector still banks continuous beam recoil
and the released Seeker salvo. Suspension replaces Bellows and Switchjack triggers
with its hold-to-store and release-to-launch ammunition. Trial and
equipped-control instructions explain these conversions.

All nine tools have a free **Try tool** option, including locked tools. These
detached ranges do not grant unlocks or overwrite Campaign progress. Saves,
blueprints and run records retain the selected tool. Archived Daily rulesets
keep their original rotations; Daily 93 introduces the nine-tool rotation.

The combat display separates charging, full pressure and mechanical cycling.
Bellows gives one quiet latch when pressure fills; Debt Collector gives one
quiet warning as its chamber approaches an automatic vent. Switchjack previews
the full player-sized landing and reports obstructions before a second press.
Its remaining bolt life and launch recovery stay visible, and a blocked jump
leaves the live bolt available to retry while it lasts.
