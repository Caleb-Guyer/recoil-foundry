# 5.49.0 — Earn Your Arsenal

The three Unconventional Arsenal tools now have separate unlock challenges that
can be earned with any weapon in Campaign or Daily.

| Weapon         | Challenge      | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bellows Cannon | Close Quarters | Defeat three enemies within 180 units in one combat room.                                                                                                                                 |
| Debt Collector | Hold the Line  | Defeat three enemies in one uninterrupted firing hold without taking damage, all in one room. Releasing fire or pausing resets the streak.                                                |
| Switchjack     | Long Haul      | Take off from solid ground, use upward recoil and travel 500 horizontal units from takeoff in one damage-free flight, then land alive. Damage, pausing and teleporting cancel the flight. |

These are best-attempt challenges. Three separate one-kill holds do not become a
three-kill streak, and several short flights do not become a long flight. The
starting-gun picker and weapon Logbook entries show the requirements and best
recorded attempt. A flight that reaches 500 units still needs its safe landing
before Switchjack unlocks.

Practice, Workshop, private previews, Try tool ranges and Quick Shift cannot earn
these unlocks. Room changes, retries and Continue reloads begin new attempts;
completed licenses and best recorded attempts stay in the profile and backups.
Challenges do not modify damage, physics, enemy AI or combat randomness.

Previously earned weapons remain available. On the first profile migration,
5.48 ownership is retained, including accomplishments recoverable from older
backups. Later Campaign clears, Press/Kiln victories and hunt victories keep
their original rewards but no longer bypass the three new challenges.

All nine tools retain their free private ranges. The three tools' mechanics and
upgrade interactions are unchanged from [Unconventional Arsenal](5.48.0.md).
