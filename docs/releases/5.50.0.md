# 5.50.0 — Melee Armory

The bottom row of the Campaign armory now holds three melee weapons. Choose **Try tool** on any card to practice before earning its license. The fitting selector lets you play each drill with the stock weapon, one fitting or all three.

| Weapon | Unlock | Exclusive fittings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arc Katana | Defeat an enemy within 120 units in Campaign or Daily, using any weapon. | Wide Guard extends the parry window. Riposte Edge gives the next slash 75% more damage after a parry. Second Beat lets one slash return two light rounds. |
| Foundry Hammer | Defeat a Campaign room boss with the Katana equipped. | Counterweight adds 60% knockback. Sweeping Face widens and lengthens the arc. Rebound Clutch cuts recovery by 25% once per swing that damages an enemy. |
| Drive Spear | Defeat the final room 20 Campaign boss with the Hammer equipped. | Quick Wind halves charge time. Deep Drive adds reach and damage to a charged thrust. Tandem Point deals 40% more damage to each enemy struck after the first. |

Return a training round to its sender, send a loose dummy sliding or pierce two aligned targets with one charged thrust. The drills use live combat and support keyboard, pointer, touch and controller input. [Katana drill](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=tools-of-trade&gun=katana&scene=range&v=1) · [Hammer drill](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=tools-of-trade&gun=hammer&scene=range&v=1) · [Spear drill](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=tools-of-trade&gun=spear&scene=range&v=1)

Strikes show their reach and can break cracked panels. A spear release just before recovery ends keeps its charge and direction. Pausing preserves an earned Katana riposte. Previously earned Bellows Cannons, Debt Collectors and Switchjacks remain available in the Workshop and Quick Shift. Existing runs can continue with their equipped tool.

Seeker Rack can now lock onto exposed mission relays, prototypes and unpowered blackout fuses. On the reported room 10 seed, a player can clear the patrol, destroy the Cooling Core relays and reach extraction. Intervening cover still blocks a lock.
