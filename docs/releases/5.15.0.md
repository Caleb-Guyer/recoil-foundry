# The Final Approach — 5.15.0

The last stretch of a new Campaign now prepares you for the Reclaimer through play. Signal Yard, Battery Walk and Reactor Verge replace eligible ordinary rooms 17–19 with authored rooftop layouts, distant aircraft and city-core machinery. A single locked lane or floor mark warns before firing, followed by a long recovery. Ground routes work with an ordinary pistol and jumps; upper decks offer recoil routes. The last route fork keeps its choice: the low service trench and high aerial crossing have different terrain and enemy rosters. Existing jobs, hunts, security encounters and other authored objectives retain their floor plans.

The rain approach has an optional wall-mounted service station before the boss trigger. Jump beside it to choose one service: recover up to 25 health, or exchange one legal fitting within your current upgrade path. A refit shows the actual complete build firing before and after; confirmation becomes available after the comparison draws. Starting weapons, fire mechanisms, fusion ingredients and upgrade prerequisites are protected. Walking past is free. Leaving an opened visit uses that shift’s service. Continue retains the same offers and decision, and Overtime has its own visit.

After a finale defeat, **Practice boss phases** opens a separate copy of the build you brought. Choose Chassis, Siege or Rupture, limited to the forms reached in that attempt. Practice skips introductions and ends when that form is destroyed. Phase-specific guidance uses the recorded cause of defeat. Retry, pause or return to the original result without changing Campaign progress, rewards or records.

New Campaigns use revision **98**. Older saved runs and pinned Dailies retain their layouts and rules. The existing three-bar finale and New Game+ difficulty are unchanged. Practice score rules remain **6**.

## Verification

Regression checks cover authored-room traversal, locked warnings, mirrored terrain and preserved special encounters; service legality, deterministic offers, saved decisions and lap separation; and reached-form gating, actual-build cloning, one-form completion and preservation of the original result. Native physics pilots and renderer checks exercise normal and mirrored rooms. These automated checks establish behavior and reachability, not human playtest results.
