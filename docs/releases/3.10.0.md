# Maintenance Shafts — 3.10.0

Rare service hatches open into two optional climbing rooms:

- **Piston shaft:** alternating banks of presses, full mechanical warnings, and sheltered landings between jumps.
- **Broken lift:** four separated elevator platforms. Ride, jump and shoot downward to transfer between them; missed jumps can be recovered from lower platforms or the floor.

Reach the summit to choose one extra compatible upgrade, then rejoin the normal route. The shaft reward does not heal. Each layout works with the starting gun; recoil upgrades and Fold can open shortcuts. No timer, objective panel or new HUD meter.

A new campaign has a 45% chance of one shaft replacing an optional challenge room in the Docks, Furnace or Rooftops. The ladder-marked upper service hatch is optional; the ordinary lower exit continues the run. Existing saves and all Daily challenges retain their original room selection. Continue restarts an unfinished shaft at its safe entrance, or restores an already-open summit reward exactly.
