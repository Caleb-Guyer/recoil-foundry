# 5.27.0 — Factory Unbound

Twenty new fittings bring the catalog to **161**. Five alternate districts and three run systems change how a shift develops. New Campaign runs use revision C107; the new Daily pool uses D91. C106 and earlier Campaigns, D90 and earlier Dailies, and all 9,216 archived maximal-build codes keep their original pools.

## How it appears in a normal run

The first room stays focused on the basics. New ordinary fittings enter after the first zone; morphs and fusions have their own later reward gates. A fitting must still meet the normal path, prerequisite and one-fusion rules. One weapon morph can be equipped at a time.

Optional service junctions can appear before ordinary encounters in rooms **7, 11, 15 and 19**. Walk to the entrance console and jump to open it, or continue past it. Authored jobs, trains, hunts and boss rooms retain their own geometry.

- **Districts:** choose between two seeded alternatives. Each replaces the room ahead and rejoins the twenty-room campaign at its exit. Consoles rotate real platforms and mirrors. Express Airlocks reward opening a bay before finishing its patrol; the surviving enemies carry forward. Rework mirrors the surviving authored terrain and prop damage from your last captured ordinary room.
- **Prototype leases:** borrow one of four morphs for two cleared Campaign rooms. The loan is separate from owned upgrades. Return it for up to 12 health and choose the ordinary fitting, or spend that fitting choice to keep it. Boss clears count; unpaid loans end at the New Game+ boundary.
- **Power routing:** Transport, Security or Reclamation adds real machinery for three eligible ordinary rooms. Safe placement reserves moving mechanisms' full paths. A room with no room for machinery does not consume a powered-room charge. Security's gun has a committed warning and can be destroyed; the shutter changes actual collision.
- **Field experiments:** fit a free prototype if you need it, perform three real actions, then choose between two permanent evolutions for this run. Complete recoil rebounds, spring launches or dodge-charge uses. Progress, outcomes, loans, circuits and reroll credits survive Continue.

Shift status is available in the expandable **Pause → Shift equipment** section. Extra equipment does not add a permanent combat HUD panel.

## Morph continuity

All six starting tools keep their real pellet counts, lanes, burst cadence and earned effects. Beam, shell, rail and steel-ball combinations retain their native payloads.

Piston Lance trades reach for a 25% projectile-payload increase and one extra penetration. Cable Saw gains penetration and one bounded half-power secondary return, after native impact falloff; Recall retains its own stronger return. Their beam forms use a fixed 35% energy increase and one extra penetration rather than extra pulses. Seeker Rack paints exposed targets before releasing a real native salvo; missiles still collide with cover. Foamcaster creates at most three brief, supported physical pieces, protecting occupied space and exits.

## Test every concept

Every link starts an isolated fixture. It cannot replace Continue, unlock a weapon, record an achievement or submit feedback. Press the retry binding (R by default) to restart. Rework's link first damages a real test room and then captures it; it does not invent a past player session.
