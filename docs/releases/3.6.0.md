# Recoil Foundry 3.6.0 — Clock Out

Overtime now has its own finale. After the Interceptor falls, the storm eases and a quiet musical theme takes over. The final exit leads to a short, calm departure bay with lights guiding you toward the lift.

Boarding ends the run and records the victory, removes the checkpoint and unlocks the existing Night Shift outfit before the presentation begins. The twelve-second elevator ride passes glimpses of all five areas before its doors open onto daylight. Skip with the on-screen button, Escape, Enter or Space; controller confirm, back and pause also skip. The run timer stops at boarding. Switching away freezes the ride, and Reduced effects replaces vertical travel with stationary dissolves.

The result screen displays your final gun, Night Shift, time and kills. An already-earned outfit stays earned; no cosmetic is equipped automatically. The normal campaign and Daily extraction retain their existing ending. The existing save warning still applies when browser storage is unavailable or another tab has conflicting changes.
