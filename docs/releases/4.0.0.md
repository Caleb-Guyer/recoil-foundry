# Factory Uprising — 4.0.0

New Campaign runs give you four decisions across the same twenty-room shift. Choose your next job after rooms 1, 5, 13 and 17; each mission rejoins the main campaign after one room. The existing high/low roads, Transmission Annex and optional encounters continue around these jobs.

Factory Shift introductions now occupy rooms 1 and 5 in new Uprising campaigns, leaving the alternate districts free for jobs. Freight surge, Power failure and the strengthened Faction conflict still change the opening fight and return in Furnace. Saved Factory plans and older replay links retain their original encounter timing.

- **Railworks:** recover a prototype, race to the evacuation platform, or unlock Convoy watch. Physical railcars move and carry riders.
- **Foundry Core:** destroy power relays, protect the crew generator, or unlock Research salvage. Core platforms move while powered and settle after sabotage.
- **Reclamation and Rooftops:** recover inventory, help crews, cut command relays or evacuate.
- **Four objectives:** break and collect a prototype, destroy two relays, activate and defend a generator for eighteen seconds, or evacuate within forty seconds. Defense waves have the existing entry warning. Generators resist damage before activation and ignore player fire. Late-room integrity scales with the patrol.

Successful jobs restore eight health, capped at full health. Stolen cargo brings extra pursuit units to later transit rooms. Core sabotage shuts down later environmental crushers/lifts, vents, coolant, fans and conductors. Crew rescue adds physical cover to later boss arenas.

Your completed jobs determine the final defense:

| Response         | Trigger                                                   | Behavior                                                  |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Isolated defense | Successful Core or command sabotage                       | Aimed volleys remain; ring attacks are replaced by fans.  |
| Security pursuit | Stolen cargo without successful sabotage                  | An Interceptor defends control.                           |
| Crew uprising    | A successful crew rescue without sabotage or stolen cargo | Crew barricades and single boss volleys.                  |
| Command overload | None of the above                                         | Fan, ring and aimed attacks cycle from the opening phase. |

Sabotage takes priority over cargo pursuit; pursuit takes priority over crew uprising. Physical crew barricades still apply when another response takes priority.

**Pause → Factory routes** shows committed jobs, outcomes and the current final response. The mission HUD and nearby interaction cues explain the current objective. **Pause → Skip current job** abandons the objective without clearing its patrol. You can also clear the patrol and leave via the exit. Failed jobs have no bonus or consequence and do not block progression.

Three persistent Campaign contracts add route choices on the next fresh Campaign:

- Complete Prototype train and Last train out to unlock **Convoy watch**.
- Complete Cut production and Keep the lights on to unlock **Research salvage**.
- Escape after three different final defenses to unlock **Last crew standing**.

Contracts and district records appear in the Logbook. Opening each new record acknowledges its own badge. Route choices, starting contract access and resolved objectives survive Continue and progress backups. Recent runs show the route and preserve it for seed replay; a replay carries its original access without awarding contracts merely for opening the link.

Existing saves keep their original campaign. Daily, Practice, Workshop, isolated previews and Overtime do not earn Campaign contract progress. Daily rules and the achievement fitting pool are unchanged.

Verification includes [the production pass](../production-pass-4.0.0.md). The supported target remains Windows desktop with keyboard and mouse; controller and touch remain experimental.
