# 5.45.0 — Special Delivery

The Scrap Courier gains working wheels, suspension and cargo clamps, local damage reactions, health-based wear, a distinct shutdown and brief inert wreckage. Its actual movement, boosts, jumps, landings, hatch opening and case recovery drive the presentation.

The dropped case remains a separate real physical object. The wreck cannot block movement or shots and grants no pickup. Escape remains a living departure with no kill or cargo. The native hatch still requires three uninterrupted seconds beside the Courier; moving it away resets that opening.

Recovered cargo gives one extra upgrade before the normal room reward. Clear bonus and following-room cues explain that the bonus grants no healing or room advance. The new Scrap Courier Logbook card uses its actual rendered portrait and explains the chase, clear-line pickup and bonus sequence. Encountering a materialized Courier discovers its own record, without pretending it is a Runner or granting the entry from an old Runner record.

Pause freezes the presentation. Reduced Effects keeps readable machinery states and steady defeated poses. Native health, speed, navigation, knockback, portals, geometry, encounter selection, pickups, rewards, audio, RNG and Continue retain their rules.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named **Test Special Delivery** button on the title. **R** restarts a running course. Movement and damage courses use the selected starting gun without upgrades. Shutdown, wreckage and cargo courses supply an upgraded tool. Each uses the actual dispatch lane and Courier AI, with unrelated patrols removed for inspection. These courses preserve saved progress, discoveries, unlocks and records.
