# 5.44.0 — Seed Lock

New Campaigns, Daily challenges and Quick Shifts separate cosmetic randomness from combat. A full particle pool, extra sparks or repeated rendering cannot change Splinter fragment directions or subsequent native enemy delays.

New runs use Campaign C111, Daily D92 or Quick Shift Q2. Existing identities retain their recorded rules: Campaign C108/C110, Daily D78–D91 and Quick Shift Q1 continue to load. Full Q1 and Q2 seeds keep their revision in the Quick Shift setup, sharing and results. Short codes and new seeds choose Q2. Old and new challenge bests remain separate; older eligible run recaps stay replayable. Checkpoint format 6 and existing storage keys remain in use.

The cosmetic stream resets at the same room-entry boundaries as combat. Native attacks, health, damage, upgrades and physics retain their rules. Pause still cancels held actions as before. The Pause comparison uses a released-input boundary and verifies that extra paused ticks freeze the simulation before resuming.

Older samples deliberately retain their original random stream, including its cosmetic sensitivity; the fix is not applied retroactively. The comparisons check every captured gameplay sample and the next combat random draw. Fingerprints summarize those comparisons and are not used as a substitute for equality. All tests are bounded private measurements, not human play sessions or a guarantee of identical floating-point physics across browsers.

Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes, fragments and unsupported link versions fail closed.

## Validation

- Actual native firing and damage regression tests compare combat under empty/full particles, cosmetic noise, repeated production rendering, Reduced Effects and safe Pause boundaries.
- Archived random draws are pinned, including the original casing and spark behavior. Room restarts, native New Game+ seed ownership and zero private progress writes are covered.
- Identity checks cover all supported Daily revisions, pinned Q1 plans and acquisition history, Q1/Q2 Continue and native results, separate records and retained recap eligibility.
- Private comparison tests require real wall-impact fragments and multiple discharges, verify checkpoint reconstruction and include an injected gameplay difference that must be detected.
