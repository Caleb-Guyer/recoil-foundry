# Combat Focus — 5.18.0

The camera follows recoil flights with bounded vertical anticipation and a restrained zoom change during fast movement. Small edge chevrons now stay with actual incoming danger instead of disappearing shortly after a projectile is fired.

## Camera framing

Choose **Camera framing** in Settings or Pause. This preference saves independently of Reduced effects.

- **Follow** anticipates climbs and descents, ignores tiny grounded motion and smoothly opens the view by up to 8% during fast movement.
- **Steady** keeps a fixed zoom and gentler vertical lead.
- **Wide** keeps a consistently wider view of the room.

Normal combat uses a slightly closer idle view, giving the adaptive camera room to widen. World bounds limit every mode: a short room or wide screen can restrict the available zoom difference. The player remains inside a safe frame during falls, portal travel and resizing. Pointer aim uses the actual camera transform. Pause freezes ordinary camera motion.

The title, authored finale shots, Clock Out, explicit archive photographs and build/support previews retain their existing framing. Tall launch and maintenance courses keep their horizontal centering while gaining vertical anticipation. One-room rematches inherit the selected camera mode.

## Offscreen warnings

Chevrons indicate committed offscreen firing lanes from supported gunner, flyer, sniper, Shutter and Strider attacks, then follow approaching hostile projectiles when their projected path threatens the player within 1.6 seconds. A slow round can remain warned after the former launch-only flash would have ended.

Warnings stop when an attack is cancelled, the threat enters view, its path misses or passes the player, or real terrain, moving cover or appropriate squad hulls block it. Frozen and staggered enemies cannot generate attack tells, but their bodies still block squad fire. Friendly, allied, reflected, spent and stasis-held shots do not generate hostile cues. Nearby volleys merge; the four most urgent directions take priority. Blackouts and finale cinematics keep their existing visibility rules.

These are presentation changes. Enemy timing, damage, movement physics, upgrades, Campaign generation, pinned Dailies and records keep their existing rules. There is no new Campaign revision or Practice scoring reset.
