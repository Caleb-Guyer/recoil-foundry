# Follow Your Goal — 5.3.0

The title's next goal now follows your attempt. Pause shows a compact progress row; select it for the original requirement and reward. Results show that attempt's goal progress beside the existing pictured rewards, including new collected upgrades retained after a loss.

## During a run

- A nearby, available hunt gets a five-second hint when it can advance your goal. Repeating an already earned hunt never counts as a different hunt.
- Upgrade choices mark the fitting you need, its prerequisite chain or a part required for the goal's achievement. The draft itself stays unchanged, and completed goals stop showing these marks.
- Progress uses actual ownership, distinct hunt awards, difficulty clears and earned licenses. Campaign and Overtime show the room reached; Gauntlet shows bosses defeated; support masteries use their existing combat counters.
- Your starting goal stays attached to the attempt even if you earn it midway through. Continue preserves its baseline. Retry starts a fresh comparison, including when it reuses the seed.

The display lives in Pause, on relevant choices and at results. It adds no permanent combat HUD. Pausing freezes the opportunity hint's timer. Daily keeps its own fixed challenge without a Campaign goal card; isolated tests and ordinary Practice cannot grant goal progress. Eligible Gauntlet and certificate attempts can show their corresponding goal without replacing the saved Campaign tracker.

## Pick the right attempt

Title goal details can open Campaign setup, the appropriate unlocked Security level, Boss Gauntlet or an unlocked trial. Opening setup does not start or replace a run. Back and Escape return to the goal. The player's first Campaign still starts directly with the pistol.

Tracking metadata participates in progress backup, restore and undo. Older saves and backups remain valid; an older Continue begins its comparison when resumed. Session-only save failures are labeled accurately rather than claiming progress was saved.
