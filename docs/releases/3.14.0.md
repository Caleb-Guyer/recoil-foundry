# Boss pursuit — 3.14.0

Bosses respond more reliably when a player stays above them, presses into a corner, or fires continuously behind cover. Health, damage, attack warnings and weapon balance are unchanged.

- After sustained camping, the Crane tries a reachable alternate strike setup when its scheduled sweep or slam is obstructed. The head physically moves into place before the full attack warning.
- The Loader commits to a usable firing position against persistent campers and tolerates small recoil bounces on a ledge. Moving players retain its usual pursuit.
- The Kiln can shell grounded platforms and find an actual standing position for an unobstructed shot. Small recoil movements no longer bait endless blocked mortars, and low debris no longer pins its pursuit.
- The Auditor follows through on platform climbs and steadies itself before its warned shots. The Welder reaches the extreme floor corners and uses its arc against overhead targets.
- The Switchboard no longer cancels attacks because a player pressed against the wall makes its escape probe overlap the boundary.
- The Turbine and Interceptor can recover from otherwise exhausted routes. Loose cover still blocks and absorbs shots while a boss works to break through it.
- Archived Daily rules 78–84 retain their previous behavior. New Dailies use rules 85. Practice uses rules 2; earlier bests remain stored separately.
