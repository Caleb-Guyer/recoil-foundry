# Recoil Foundry 3.9.1 — Hot Work

The Welder's equipment was supposed to stay decommissioned.

**Hot Work:** destroy both of The Welder's barricades during one deployment, then defeat it in a real run. The commendation permanently unlocks:

- **Forgehand:** a scorched welding jacket, copper stitching and a raised visor.
- **Kiln:** a dark steel gun finish whose three recessed vents briefly glow after firing, including beams. Reduced effects soften the glow.
- **Decommission Notice 031:** a new maintenance record by M. Vale in the logbook.

Equip either reward independently in **Workshop → Appearance**. Both are cosmetic; neither changes damage, movement or hitboxes. The challenge adds no HUD.

Cancelled, expired, replaced and enemy-destroyed walls do not count. Separate deployments cannot combine. Once a pair is destroyed, later attacks in that fight do not erase it. Death or restarting the encounter clears unfinished challenge progress. Practice, Workshop and test links award nothing permanent. Earned rewards travel with progress backups.
