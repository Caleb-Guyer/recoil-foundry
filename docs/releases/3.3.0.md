# Crosswind — 3.3.0

Overtime Cooling now has six authored layouts built around sustained airflow and recoil movement:

- **Intake shafts:** two broad updrafts between raised firing positions.
- **Cooling towers:** staggered platforms with alternating lift windows.
- **Bypass duct:** the lower route, with a short lift shaft and a crossflow corridor.
- **Exhaust skyway:** the upper route, with high platforms and a lateral current above them.
- **Intake manifold:** the Condenser arena, with twin updraft escape routes.
- **Crossflow chamber:** the Turbine arena, with lift shafts and a crossing current below the platforms.

Recessed fans spin up with a full 1.25-second visual/audio warning, then ramp into a 3.6-second gust. Jumping enters the airflow; ordinary grounded steering and bullet/beam aim remain unchanged. Wind adds momentum without erasing a stronger recoil boost. It moves lighter airborne enemies and loose crates while respecting physical cover. It never damages or interrupts bosses. Finite waves include coordinated enemy pairs, and fans power down after clearance.

The HUD remains minimal. Direction marks and warning lanes remain visible with Reduced effects. Every room has a route using ordinary jumps, even with ventilation off.

New Overtime entries use revision 3, retaining the earlier Docks and Furnace remixes. Existing unversioned, revision-1 and revision-2 saves keep their original generation. Later areas, ordinary campaign, Daily, upgrades and earned boss Practice retain their rules. The Practice ruleset remains 1. Rollbacks must preserve revision-3 checkpoint decoding and generation.
