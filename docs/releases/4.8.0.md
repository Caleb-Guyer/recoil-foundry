# Boss Gauntlet — 4.8.0

Your first Campaign victory opens **Practice → Boss Gauntlet**. Begin with full health and an unmodified starting gun you have unlocked. Choose between two opponents before each of five fights:

1. The Loader or the Crane.
2. The Press or the Kiln.
3. The Condenser or the Turbine.
4. The Sorter or the Reclaimer.
5. The Interceptor or the Welder.

Each opponent uses its actual authored arena, physical cover, machinery and attack warnings. Boss health is tuned for this shorter run's four fitting opportunities; Campaign, ordinary boss Practice and Overtime retain their existing health and rules. The choice screen photographs the actual boss artwork. All 32 boss routes are available after Campaign victory; individual alternate-boss victories are not required.

Health carries into the next arena. After the first four clears, choose one of three legal upgrades or recover up to **30 health**. Repairs replace an upgrade and cannot be taken at full health. There is no entry heal or automatic room-completion heal. Healing from an earned gun fitting keeps its usual effect. A choice can be spent once. The gun stays with you through all five rounds.

Combat time, shots and hits accumulate across the run. Pause and the between-fight stops do not advance the timer. Completed routes keep separate best times for the same starting gun and boss sequence, with fewer hits and then fewer shots deciding equal times. The setup screen includes expandable route/build records. Records survive progress export, restore and Undo restore; older backups migrate to an empty Gauntlet collection.

Finish all five to earn **Five Machines Down** and the **Victor** outfit, with its dark green jacket, gold cuffs and five chest marks. The first completion shows a reward card with the real outfit artwork. Equip it in Workshop → Appearance; its commendation and photograph also appear in the Logbook. Repeated completions do not report a new reward.

Death ends the attempt. **Restart Gauntlet** begins all five rounds again with full health and an unmodified gun; it cannot reset one fight for free health. Menu abandons the attempt. Gauntlet fights do not pay Campaign upgrades, boss-victory unlocks, weapon milestones, Daily scores or combat commendations. Your Campaign Continue and reward ledger remain available. The sole new completion reward is awarded only after a legitimate full Gauntlet.

Isolated playtests use `?test=gauntlet`, with optional `&gun=shotgun` or `&gun=nailgun`. These links let you inspect the real flow with ordinary controls while keeping records, rewards and progress untouched. They do not unlock the mode or a gun.
