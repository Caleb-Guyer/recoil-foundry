# 5.37.0 — Support Systems

Repairers, Relays, Fabricators and Sentries now have distinct mechanical rigs, local hit reactions, shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage. Their actual support and combat states drive the presentation.

## Working support crew

- **Repairer:** articulated tools and a pump follow the real repair link and accepted pulses. Its reservoir shows the actual remaining repair supply, including its final pulse.
- **Relay:** three charge cells and induction coils follow charging, readiness and actual partner discharges. Expiring or breaking a link does not imitate a shot.
- **Fabricator:** assembly arms follow the real supported frame and build progress. Interrupting assembly drops an unfinished machine; destroying its builder cuts power to its surviving Sentries.
- **Sentry:** deployment, tracking, lock and recoil follow the actual turret. Firing keeps its native muzzle and warning lines; disruption does not imitate recoil.

Accepted damage produces body-local reactions and wear follows real health. Defeated sources have distinct mechanical failures and unlit settling wreckage. Power-loss shutdowns retain the child’s actual assembly state. Native links, warnings, damage, timing, collision, rewards and saves retain their rules. Rendering uses detached snapshots and adds no physics bodies.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and damage-reaction courses use your selected starting tool; shutdown and wreckage courses supply an upgraded tool. Wreckage subjects begin over a real raised deck. A distant real guard keeps the room active while a shutdown settles. Tests preserve Continue, unlocks, discoveries and saved records.

Repairer courses start with a wounded real partner so its existing repair cycle has work to do. Sentry courses begin with a real turret created and completed through its actual Fabricator controller, with its owner still present.

## Construction and power loss

These courses use the actual builder and owned turret. Interruption starts with an unfinished frame: hit its builder or destroy the frame. Power loss starts with a completed turret: destroy the Fabricator first. Removing its child grants no extra kill or reward.

- Fabricator: [Interrupted construction](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support-systems&unit=fabricator&scene=interruptions&v=1) · [Power-loss shutdown](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support-systems&unit=fabricator&scene=power-loss&v=1).
- Sentry: [Interrupted construction](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support-systems&unit=sentry&scene=interruptions&v=1) · [Power-loss shutdown](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support-systems&unit=sentry&scene=power-loss&v=1).

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` changes the private tool. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes, invalid construction subjects and unsupported link versions fail closed.
