# Recoil Foundry 3.19.0 — Foundry Archive

The collection and notification portion of [issue #7](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/7).

The Logbook uses a responsive icon grid and details panel. Search discovered names and filter by discovery, locked challenges, unread entries or upgrade family. Keyboard arrows move through the grid; Enter opens an entry. Controller navigation supports the family selector and document scrolling. On narrow screens the details follow the grid and the header keeps Back accessible.

Equipment has a slot for each of the 108 upgrades. Seeing a reward offer recovers its icon, function and authored record independently of collecting it for the Workshop. Unencountered upgrades use silhouettes and anonymous slot labels. The Machines collection includes all 30 enemy types, three elite variants and three mutations, with individual artwork, behavior guides and variant records. Sites cover the five main zones and two concealed special sites; their names and records appear only after discovery. Documents and commendations use the same grid. Revealed pending commendations have a question-mark icon and their exact requirement.

New entries notify on the title’s Logbook button, category tab and tile. Only explicitly opening that entry acknowledges it. Opening the Logbook, changing category, filtering, searching or moving focus leaves unread entries intact. Earning a previously read pending commendation creates a fresh notification. Preview and test views do not acknowledge real discoveries.

The atomic progress profile now carries a validated optional archive category. Legacy profiles and backups migrate their existing records as read. Sightings and acknowledgements persist through reload, Continue, backup, restore and undo without granting Workshop ownership or changing equipped appearance. Runtime sighting reports occur once per materialized enemy per room, excluding allies, relay objectives and unfinished arrivals; Practice, Workshop and test runs cannot grant sightings.

Validation: 2,212 automated tests passed across 141 files, excluding the existing expensive `max-combos` exhaustive physics catalog. Production compilation passed. Browser checks exercise individual badge acknowledgment and reload, enemy variant lore, keyboard navigation, silhouettes, filters, and narrow-screen layout. The achievement-based upgrade unlocks and new mobility/electrical abilities are the next part of the implementation.
