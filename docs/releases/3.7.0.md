# Recoil Foundry 3.7.0 — Overtime balance

Overtime reinforcements now enter in staggered pairs, with a different rhythm in each area. Authored squad partners arrive together. Openings bring forward one suitable pressure enemy when needed, while elites and coordinated squads remain in reserve. The same units use their existing safe entrances; no extra health or enemies are added.

Boss support becomes eligible sooner and starts its warning during an idle or recovery window. Later pairs follow with their own complete warning and arrival grace. Occupied entrances still wait or relocate with a fresh warning. Enemy attack telegraphs retain their full duration. These encounter changes apply to current Overtime layouts; earlier saved layout revisions keep their original timing.

Three-card Overtime rewards now include a legal follow-up to a component you already own whenever one remains available. The other choices stay open, earned salvage stays offered, and rerolls respect their exclusions. First-lap and Daily rewards keep their existing selection rules.
