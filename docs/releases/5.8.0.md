# Room remaster & combat clarity — 5.8.0

Six existing room designs now offer more deliberate choices between a clear lower route and broad upper firing decks. Low steps provide recovery after a missed launch, while gaps between decks let you escape a firing line. Cover and patrol positions leave room around entrances, exits and landing surfaces. Small painted brackets mark the real upper platform ends.

- **Overpass:** offset overhead decks above a lower loading route.
- **Cargo switchyard:** two stepped cargo banks and an open center launch.
- **Warm exchange:** separated patrol heights and a high route past the guard.
- **Upper return:** connected firing heights with gaps for dropping out of a locked line.
- **Scrap bridge:** two broken bridge decks above a ground escape and salvageable barricades.
- **Roof braces:** broad roof landings above a lower recovery route.

The first room keeps its onboarding layout. Fresh Campaign seeds use revision 92; existing Campaign Continue saves and Daily room layouts keep their earlier design. These are replacements for existing designs in the room pool, so a normal run will not necessarily encounter all six. Machine and teamwork introductions remain in place.

During busy combat, the actual player outfit draws above friendly beams, arcs, blasts and trails, with a subtle dark separation stroke when effects become dense. Ordinary committed attack warnings join the existing machine warnings and hostile projectiles above friendly effects. Visible friendly effects ease their particles, beam halos, ammunition glow and cosmetic camera movement as density increases. Beam cores and real shots remain drawn; damage, collision, ammunition colors and blackout illumination rules retain their behavior. Reduced effects remains available in Settings.

## Play the rooms

Each link opens an isolated preset. Choose **Test [room name]**, then use normal controls. **R** retries the same room and build. Presets grant no rewards or unlocks and do not replace your Continue save. The later presets start at their listed room with an unmodified pistol unless a build is named; the two combination links include legal upgrades for that stage.
