# Feel Every Shot — 4.3.0

The Recoil shotgun has a deeper blast, a visible sliding pump with a mechanical return sound, and compact directional pellet impacts. The Burst nailgun has a sharp mechanical report for every round of its burst, visible metal projectiles with matching light, and spent nails that stick briefly into scenery. Embedded nails follow the position and rotation of moving objects, disappear when the object breaks, and fade after 1.8 seconds. Ricocheting and piercing nails keep flying; enemies do not receive scenery decals. Converted ammunition retains its own visual identity.

Player takeoffs stretch the body, falling changes the leg pose, landings compress according to impact speed, and shots produce a small body recoil. Patrol machines have articulated feet, flyers have moving rotors, and gun machines brace their receiver before firing. Directional damage reactions complement the existing armor flashes, boss animations and attack warnings.

These changes affect presentation: damage, projectile trajectories, firing intervals, physics hulls, recoil velocity and weapon unlocks remain unchanged. Effects use simulation time and do not consume gameplay randomness. Pause and hitstop freeze the new poses and pending pump; room changes and run endings clear effects. Effect counts are bounded, nearby pellet impacts combine, and cosmetic marks fade near hostile projectiles. Reduced effects suppresses body motion and keeps pump movement and impacts small; attack warning geometry remains visible.

Explicit isolated playtests use the normal controls and renderer without changing progress: `?test=combat-feel&gun=shotgun`, `?test=combat-feel&gun=nailgun` and `?test=combat-feel&gun=pistol`.
