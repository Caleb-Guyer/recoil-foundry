# Loader ledge fix — 3.13.1

Fixes [issue #4](https://github.com/Caleb-Guyer/recoil-foundry/issues/4): standing on a platform and banking shots off the ceiling could leave the Loader hopping harmlessly underneath.

- When a player stays on a ledge, the Loader commits to a route around it, clears its full hull over the edge, and climbs up before resuming its normal attacks. Brief jumps retain the usual response.
- If the player drops back to the floor, the Loader follows around the edge, including any hanging cargo that catches its descent.
- Narrow crates and fuel canisters no longer leave its tread support undetected and stall its movement.
- Platform and prop collisions remain physical. The charge retains its full warning and exposed crash recovery; ordinary movement and jumping can evade it. Bank Shot damage and recoil are unchanged.
