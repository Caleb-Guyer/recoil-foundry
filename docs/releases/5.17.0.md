# Hear the Foundry — 5.17.0

Combat sounds now carry their actual world positions. Enemy warnings and shots, reinforcement entrances, machinery and impacts move through a restrained stereo field relative to the player. Distance gently reduces their level; attack warnings keep an audible floor. Two enemies making the same warning retain separate emitter identities, while repeated sounds from one emitter keep their normal cooldown. Existing attack timing, damage, gun behavior and progression are unchanged.

Impacts add short material layers for armored surfaces, metal bodies, breakable cover, powered cabinets and destructible terrain. The existing gun sounds, music, warning priority and ducking remain in the mix. Source and voice budgets bound dense fights, and old room sounds are released on a room change. Pause, hidden or unfocused pages, mute and reset stop the relevant voices and delayed effects.

Five quiet procedural soundscapes give the factory an audible presence: loading-bay machinery, furnace pressure, coolant pumps, reclamation belts and rooftop wind. Nearby moving equipment changes mechanical activity. Blackouts, cut shutdown circuits and successful power sabotage affect the powered layer; clearing enemies does not pretend to turn machinery off. Room air and outdoor wind remain when power is cut. The cinematic rain approach and finale keep their existing score and quiet moments.

**Mono audio** in Settings centers the entire mix, including effects, rain and music, and is saved with the existing audio preferences. Effects volume controls ambience and combat sounds together; music retains its separate control. Private room rematches and support drills use their own active listener rather than the original paused run.

There is no new Campaign revision or Practice scoring reset. Saved runs, records, unlocks and pinned Dailies keep their rules. This update applies to their sound presentation too.

## Verification

Regression coverage exercises real emitter positions and identities, simultaneous same-kind warnings, material damage events, reinforcement arrivals, power state and Continue, spatial voice limits, changing listeners, live mono changes, delayed-note cancellation, context failure and missing-panner fallback. The Soundcheck also offers native browser OfflineAudioContext renders to verify left/right channel energy, centered mono output, mute/pause silence, nonzero material effects and powered/unpowered ambience. These are technical audio checks rather than listening-session or human playtest claims.

Spatial routing and offline checks use the browser’s standard [Web Audio API](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/).

Final validation: **3,305 regression tests passed**, including 75 focused audio tests. The production TypeScript/Vite build passed. Native browser renders passed all **21 audio cases** and **five powered/unpowered comparisons**, with finite output, correct stereo direction, matched mono channels, and silence after mute or pause.
