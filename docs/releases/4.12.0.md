# Keep Your Build — 4.12.0

Cutting Torch now keeps the shape of the gun you built. Scattershot becomes **five separate beams** instead of being folded into one wider beam. Prism Array splits every beam into two at 60% power each: **ten rays in two fans** with Scattershot. The starting shotgun retains five native rays, or nine with Scattershot; the nailgun keeps its three-pulse cadence.

Charge Lens releases the whole spread as charged lances. Burst and Pulse Chamber fire the spread on each pulse. Backfire mirrors the full pattern behind you. Splinter releases three smaller rounds from each ray's first contact per pulse, with the same total fragment energy divided among the rays. Banks, penetration and portal traversal use each ray's actual path. Resonator repeats all forward rays that really crossed a portal in one delayed discharge, respecting the remaining range and cover at the time of the echo.

Damage is distributed among the rays rather than multiplied by the new visuals. A narrow target takes damage from the rays touching it; closer or larger targets can catch more of the spread. Stored cells, Landing shot, recoil and shared contact recoveries stay attached to the discharge. Breaking cover cannot let another ray shoot through it in the same step. Thin ray art and a shared lighting budget keep wide fans readable.

Upgrade descriptions and comparisons show the combined pattern in either acquisition order, including the actual number of forward and rear beams. Existing saves, blueprints, Workshop builds and replays use the updated firing behavior while retaining their upgrades and starting gun.

New Dailies use ruleset **87**. Archived Daily links, Continue saves and their best-time identities keep the previous wide-beam mechanics; their upgrade descriptions and live comparisons use those rules too.
