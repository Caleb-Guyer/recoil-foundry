# Earn Your Tools — 4.2.0

The first Campaign begins with the Service pistol, without a weapon selection screen. Later attempts display all three guns and their unlock requirements. Clear the Campaign to unlock the Recoil shotgun; complete Overtime to unlock the Burst nailgun. Workshop choices and imported blueprints respect these locks. Existing Continue saves retain their gun, and past victories migrate to the appropriate unlocks; entering Overtime alone does not unlock the nailgun. Daily challenges retain their shared fixed loadout as loaned equipment.

End screens now collect newly earned starting guns, appearance options, achievement fittings and completed contracts into individual reward cards. Outfit and finish cards show the actual game artwork. A saved reward ledger keeps awards earned before Continue and resets for a new run. Repeated awards do not add duplicate cards, and previously owned rewards are not reported as new. Maintenance certification also displays its Servicewear outfit card.

The Logbook now photographs machines and variants through the production enemy renderer, and sites through the complete world renderer with their authored geometry and scenery. Alternate districts load their actual rooms. These static images are cached, do not simulate gameplay, and do not award discoveries. Commendations display their real outfit and gun-finish rewards; the Equipment section includes weapon unlock records after the first run.

Isolated ending previews show sample cards without changing progress. Progress backups include weapon milestones and the active run’s reward ledger, while older backups remain supported.
