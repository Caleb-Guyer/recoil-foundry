# Recoil Foundry 3.8.0 — The Welder

A rare machine is still repairing the factory after the shift ends.

The Welder can replace one coordinated squad in a new Overtime run. It marks short seams across floors and nearby walls before they ignite, welds temporary barricades, and fires a committed spread of molten bolts. Every major attack ends with its cooling shutters open: the exposed assembly takes extra damage. Its tracked chassis, welding mask and electrical warning sound distinguish it from ordinary troops.

Barricades have a visible construction warning, can be shot apart, and expire after six seconds. They cancel if their space becomes occupied. The Welder cannot seal the entrance or exit, and its seams and barriers disappear when it dies. Destroying its renewable cover does not generate scrap or demolition rewards.

Defeating it earns an additional upgrade selection before the room's regular reward. The choices use your current build's legal follow-ups, including its normal path and branch restrictions. Continue preserves the defeat and pending reward. The extra upgrade can only be claimed once.

Its maintenance record and Practice fight unlock after a real victory. Practice recreates the encountered room with the Welder alone. First laps, Daily challenges, major boss rooms and existing Overtime checkpoints keep their previous encounter selection.
