# Your Run, Explained — 4.14.0

The Campaign and Daily end screens now show **What carried your run?**: a compact combat report with the finished gun, the outfit you wore and up to three connections between its fittings. Expand **Recorded results** for the run's time, enemies defeated and relevant upgrade counters. Recent Runs preserves the report beside its existing blueprint, Workshop and replay controls.

Pictures use the same weapon and outfit drawing code as gameplay. Each finished report records its appearance, so changing your equipped finish later does not repaint past runs. Build highlights use the actual starting tool, combined pellet and lane counts, rear fire and preserved beam rules. A Scattershot and Prism build shows ten forward beams; a shotgun's native pellets change that count. Highlights explain the final owned build rather than claiming a measured damage ranking.

The counters come from actual simulation events:

- **Heat transfers:** a positive stored relay actually consumed for another tracked target. Creating a relay or letting it expire does not count.
- **Overkill charges:** one positive reserve payment per discharge. A whole pellet volley or ten-ray beam payment counts once; empty reserves do not count.
- **Scrap Armor:** ordinary hostile bullets actually absorbed by a plate. Collecting a plate, invulnerability, heavy ammunition and environmental hits do not count.
- **Bloodwork:** health actually restored by credited kills, after the health cap. Full-health kills do not invent healing; room repairs are separate.

Totals survive room changes and Continue. Fresh runs, retries and new test or Workshop sessions reset them. Optional reporting data is bounded and validated; malformed metadata does not discard an otherwise valid checkpoint or run. Older recaps identify missing results and appearance. Resuming an older checkpoint without counters marks its subsequent report as partial. Backups preserve the new data. Upgrade counters for never-owned fittings stay concealed.

This update does not change gameplay balance, reward sequences, Daily identities or save versions.

## Test it

- [Open an isolated end-screen example](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=presentation&scene=escape&v=4.14.0). Its scripted result demonstrates the actual report UI; upgrade counters start at zero. It cannot grant rewards or replace your saved run.
- [Play all four support upgrades with ten beams](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support&build=combined&v=4.14.0). Click **Test All four · ten beams**. On defeat, the report shows the counters from that fight. **R** retries with fresh counters, leaving your Campaign progress untouched.
- [Play a steel-ball Overkill build](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=support&build=mass&v=4.14.0). The report preserves its ball volley and counts shared reserve payments.

Finish a normal Campaign or Daily attempt to save a report, then open **Recent runs** from the title screen or the expanded end-screen recap to revisit it.
