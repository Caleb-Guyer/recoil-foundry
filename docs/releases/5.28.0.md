# 5.28.0 — The Reclaimer’s Arsenal

The final boss gains a distinct mechanic in each physical form, carried into harder New Game+ encounters. These mechanics build on 5.27.1’s physical pursuit and anti-camping repair.

## Three forms

- **Chassis — committed counter-dash.** The Reclaimer marks a fixed dash path, commits its real collision body, then leaves a recovery window. Bait the path, move clear and punish the landing. Cover remains physical.
- **Siege — aircraft and armor.** A traveling aircraft drops bombs into warned impact zones. Sustained damage breaks the frame’s visible armor and exposes its core for a temporary damage window. Shots, beams and earned damage effects share the same armor interaction; your gun and upgrades remain intact.
- **Rupture — rotating core and collapsing decks.** Core beams turn through the arena with a visible windup. Marked platforms crumble out of the actual collision layout in stages, changing where you can stand and fire. Three warned collapses leave two real shelves and the permanent floor available for recovery.
- **New Game+.** Stronger combinations extend these patterns with full warnings and deliberate recovery windows. The form-specific tests use the legal fittings earned through the second lap.

Visual warnings use the game’s actual positions and damage geometry. Native mechanical sounds follow dash commitments, aircraft, armor breaks and core attacks; mute, mono and Reduced effects continue to apply. Cutscenes and the compact red health bar keep their existing presentation.

Successful isolation still helps: longer signature warnings and recovery, fewer smaller bombs, lighter Siege armor with a longer exposure window, and a slower core sweep with later platform collapses. New Game+ retains its own ordinary factory defense.

Each isolated link starts directly in the named full-health form with a legal, retained Service pistol build, real collision and ordinary attack warnings. New Game+ adds legal second-lap fittings.
