# 3.16.0 — Security Levels

Clear the factory once to reveal **Security** beneath Play. Choose a level before starting another full twenty-room campaign. Existing recorded victories count.

- **Security I — Reinforced:** selected regular rooms gain coordinated shield squads. The members use existing spawn anchors and warned reinforcement doors; defeating the shield bearer breaks their coordination.
- **Security II — Adapted:** keeps Reinforced encounters and adds alternating boss counterattack combinations. Amber aim lines track briefly, lock for 0.45 seconds, then fire after a full 1.05-second warning. Forked volleys and three-shot fans follow different bosses' primary attacks. A recovery opening follows each counter; every other primary attack retains its normal recovery.
- **Security III — Redline:** keeps Adapted encounters and introduces five machinery checkpoints, mirrored with the run: Inspection aisle, Thermal inspection, Cooling checkpoint, Recovery inspection and Roof inspection. Presses, shootable steam valves, coolant lifts, magnets and fans share their existing physical rules. Special encounters and the optional Annex keep their authored rooms.

Each clearance unlocks the next level. Your fastest first-lap clearance is saved separately for Standard and each Security Level. Taking the Overtime elevator banks that clearance before the next lap; Security stays attached to the run. Clearing Redline earns the **Redline outfit** and a Dispatch report in the Logbook. The reward changes appearance only.

The selector, current-run marker, result recap and Progress backup identify the selected level. Continue, retry, history replay and progress restore retain it. No general enemy-health, weapon-damage or projectile-speed multiplier is added. Standard, Daily, ordinary Practice and existing checkpoints retain their rules.
