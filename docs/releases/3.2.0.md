# Recoil Foundry 3.2.0 — Pressure Shift

Overtime Furnace now has its own pressure-driven encounters. Watch the pipework, shoot a charged valve, and use the safe interval after its jet to cross or change elevation.

- **Split boilers:** divided firing lanes with alternating steam shafts.
- **Relief gallery:** three staggered pressure lanes between raised firing positions.
- **Service tunnel:** the lower route, with low ceilings, jumpable cover and an ambush pair.
- **Flue walk:** the upper route, with higher platforms and airborne flankers over the steam lanes.
- **Pressure press:** a remixed Press arena with three long jets that can expose its armor.
- **Boiler floor:** a remixed Kiln arena where steam openings compete with mortar and floor-fire warnings.

Charged valves glow; a shot starts the full 1.15-second warning before a half-second burst. Hot jets damage and push the player and ordinary enemies, and solid cover blocks the flow. An early release gives a 5.6-second recharge interval before the valve becomes ready again. Hostile bullets cannot trigger it. After combat, hot jets shut down for a safe exit.

A manually released jet that catches the Press or Kiln opens its armor for 1.6 seconds, indicated by a pale green core and a mechanical sound. This does not cancel its attack. The eight-second cooldown is shared across all valves, so shooting several valves cannot chain permanent openings. Automatic cycles do not open boss armor, and jets do not move bosses or deal direct boss damage.

The HUD stays minimal. Each room uses one machinery family, with permanently reachable floor routes, reserved enemy entrances and finite reinforcement waves. Layouts mirror with the seed. All normal campaign, Daily, ordinary Practice and upgrade rules remain unchanged.

New Overtime entries receive generation revision 2, including the previous Docks remixes. Saved revision-1 runs keep their old Furnace rooms, and older unversioned Overtime saves keep their original generation. Later regions are unchanged.
