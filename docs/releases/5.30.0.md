# 5.30.0 — Build in Motion

Your equipped build is easier to understand during a real run. This update adds explanations and measured feedback to the existing mechanics; firing rules, weapon balance, Campaign generation and pinned Daily identities retain their revisions.

## Your build now

Expand **Your build now** in Pause to read what your equipped morph, fusion or support fitting does, how to trigger it, and its actual readiness. Waiting, charging, ready, active and blocked states come from the live native controllers. Only equipped fittings and an actually leased morph appear; the panel does not reveal uncollected future content. Details remain behind one disclosure so the combat HUD stays clear.

Seeker locks, captured payloads, Slip Gauge bursts, remote muzzles, armed recoil paths, banked jumps and nearby spring pads can show one quiet action cue beside the player. A cue appears only when the corresponding native action is usable. It does not consume charges or invoke the fitting. Threat warning layers retain priority; menus, defeat, hitstop and visual finale transitions suppress the cue. Reduced Effects keeps the same steady information.

## Measured highlights

The end-of-run combat report now explains the four Factory Unbound morphs and six fusions with their actual triggers and preserved firing patterns. Up to three measured highlights show real actions such as captured rounds, remote volleys, released jump reserves, recoil rebounds and fusion activations. Expanded recorded results include the equipped mechanisms that were measured at zero too.

Counters are recorded where an effect actually succeeds. A pellet spread or repeated render frame cannot inflate one discharge into several activations. The report does not estimate aggregate DPS or attribute overlapping damage to individual upgrades. The existing Heat Relay, Overkill Bank, Scrap Armor and Bloodwork results and mastery rewards keep their meanings.

New counters travel through Continue, Recent Runs and progress backups. Older reports distinguish unavailable activity from measured zero; new activity recorded after loading an older run is explicitly partial. Malformed optional new metadata cannot prevent an otherwise valid save from loading.

## Practice the equipped gun

Choose **Practice** from an owned fitting’s Pause card to open its suggested native Workshop scenario with your current gun and fittings. Grouped targets, Incoming fire, Cover & banks and the Recoil course exercise actual damage, defense, destruction and movement.

Practice uses a separate disposable physics world. The original run stays paused, including its health, live enemies, charges, room state and checkpoint. Reset restores the private range; Return takes you back to the same explanation. Private exercises grant no discoveries, licenses, rewards, Campaign progress or saved records. Legacy and leased equipment stay represented in the copied gun.

## Validation

Native checks cover controller-derived readiness without state mutation, cue suppression and priority, bounded activation counters, complete-discharge deduplication, optional metadata compatibility, Continue/history/backup copies, all fixture/tool combinations and private practice isolation. Browser verification is performed with Sound and Music off. All existing regular and maximal-build CI checks remain required for deployment.

Local validation passed **4,285 regular tests**, **65 final focused tests**, the production build and diff checks. [Validation scope](../validation/build-in-motion-5.30.0.json) separates native regressions and muted agent-controlled browser checks from human playtests.
