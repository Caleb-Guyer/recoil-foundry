# 5.33.0 — The Operator

The worker now has articulated movement, a grip that fits the equipped tool, contact-driven surface sounds and an animated dressing-room preview. These are presentation changes: collision, movement forces, shot origins, damage, upgrade rules and unlock requirements keep their existing behavior.

## Move with the gun

Running and reversing use distinct leg poses. Launching, rising, falling and landing follow the actual movement state; recoil travels through the shoulder and stance. Feet, knees and gloved arms keep the familiar bright visor readable against the factory.

Two-handed grips follow actual aim, the native weapon and equipped conversions. The weapon keeps its existing render transform and muzzle position. The player is still vulnerable through the same native collision body.

## Hear the contact

Solid metal, thin grating and coolant use distinct step and landing details. Moving support is accounted for: being carried while standing still does not become a walking loop. Braking can produce a short scrape. Coolant detail requires actual supported contact with the native pool.

The new effects follow the Sound toggle, effects volume and mono controls. They do not use the music channel or autoplay in Appearance. Pause, menus, standing still and airborne movement do not generate footsteps; room changes and teleports reset the cadence.

## Inspect the outfit

Open **Workshop → Appearance** to preview the selected outfit in motion. Pose and tool controls are local to the preview. Existing earned-item requirements, equipment choices and notices remain in place. The preview does not fire a real shot, play sound or advance a run.

Reduced Effects keeps a steady, readable presentation. Closing the menu or switching away from Appearance stops its preview work.

## Private tests

Select the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts the private course. Test links leave Continue, unlocks, discoveries and run records untouched. A distant native patrol keeps the movement course active; clearing it ends that private test normally.
