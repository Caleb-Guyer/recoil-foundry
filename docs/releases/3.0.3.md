# Recoil Foundry 3.0.3 — Your gun, their arena

- Choose **Preset** or **Workshop build** when selecting a defeated boss in Practice.
- Fit upgrades you have discovered, using normal prerequisites and path rules. Each fight caps the gun at its usual pre-boss upgrade count: 3, 7, 11, 15 or 19.
- **Retry** keeps the chosen build, including the starting gun. **Edit build** is available after an attempt and from Pause; starting the edited fight restores full health and resets the arena.
- Practice drafts are kept during this session, separately from your saved Workshop gun. Cancelling an edit leaves the current fight unchanged.
- Fixed importing progress backups after discovering the Annex, including earned Switchboard victories.

Bosses still require a recorded victory to unlock. Practice never awards discoveries, victories, commendations or campaign progress. Campaign saves and Daily rules are unchanged.

[Play on itch.io](https://caleb-guyer.itch.io/recoil-foundry) · [Play on GitHub Pages](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/)

Open **Practice → a defeated boss → Workshop build** to try it. If no bosses have been defeated in this browser, Practice stays hidden.
