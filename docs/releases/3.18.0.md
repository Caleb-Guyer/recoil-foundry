# 3.18.0 — Factory Shifts

Fresh normal runs gain one of three factory conditions:

- **Freight surge:** a moving freight crossing in room two and the boarding lift in room six.
- **Power failure:** restore the lights by shooting a lit power box in rooms two and six.
- **Faction conflict:** a small three-against-three crew fight in room two, followed by a larger battle in Furnace. Blue crews fight red crews; surviving allies leave when combat ends.

A brief HUD description explains the condition and the current encounter. The first room keeps its existing teaching layout. Signature encounters are separated by ordinary rooms, routes and bosses. Most runs also get a contrasting single-room Blackout, Turf War or Lockdown in Reclamation. These events no longer fill three consecutive rooms in new normal runs.

The first two upgrade screens guarantee an eligible option that changes firing or movement, while retaining the early power safeguard. Rerolls preserve both safeguards when candidates remain; builds, prerequisites, exclusions and salvage rules still apply.

Fresh random normal starts and retries avoid the previous condition. Explicit seeds reproduce their condition and encounter plan. Continue, progress backups and run recaps retain the selected plan or condition; cached faction rerolls carry forward to ordinary rooms. Legacy recap replays use their original encounter rules. Dispatch offices can appear in later eligible rooms so the new opening never removes their documents.

Existing saves retain their original rules. Daily rules 78–85, Practice, Workshop, isolated tests and Overtime encounter rules are unchanged. No new account, currency or permanent combat bonus is added.

Validation covers seeded scheduling, actual encounter selection, ordinary routes, progress and reward reloads, malformed saves, cache rerolls, old profiles, all supported Daily versions and starting-gun combat simulations. Browser verification uses a separate local origin with disposable progress. These checks establish software behavior; player feedback is still needed to assess how distinct the runs feel.

The regression run passed **2,194 tests**, including 20 Factory Shifts tests and full campaign clears for all three conditions. The freight campaign took the Annex branch; the other two took Cooling. All six opening and Furnace signature simulations also cleared with the starting gun. TypeScript, the production build, formatting and browser HUD/reload checks passed, with no browser console errors. The exhaustive `max-combos.test.ts` Workshop stress matrix was not rerun; Workshop's firing simulation is unchanged.
