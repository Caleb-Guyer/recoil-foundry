# 5.38.0 — Hazard Crew

The Scrapper, Harpooner and Sapper now have working mechanical rigs, localized damage reactions, distinct shutdowns and brief nonblocking wreckage. Their actual grabs, launches and interruptions drive the presentation.

## Dangerous work

- **Scrapper:** an articulated clamp grips and lifts the real crate, braces to aim and opens on its actual throw. Breaking its hold produces a different recovery from a successful release.
- **Harpooner:** its winch and cable guide follow the native launch, reel and cable tension. Breaking the shootable anchor releases the line and settles the mechanism.
- **Sapper:** the loading mechanism and launcher follow aiming and accepted charge launches. Interrupting an aim or failing a blocked launch does not imitate a shot.

Accepted hits produce local reactions at the actual hull contact; casing wear follows real health. Each defeated machine has its own mechanical shutdown and unlit wreck. Wreckage adds no collision bodies or rewards. Existing warnings, real crates, harpoons, charges, damage, AI timing and save formats keep their rules. Released charges retain their native fuse and knock-away behavior after their owner is defeated.

## Private inspection courses

Choose the named test on the title screen. **R** restarts it. Animation and reaction courses use the selected starting tool; shutdown and wreckage courses provide an upgraded tool. Wreckage courses place both you and the subject on a real raised deck. A distant guard keeps the room active while the wreck settles. These tests preserve Continue, unlocks, discoveries and saved records.

## Interrupted mechanisms

These courses use the real controllers and counterplay:

- [Scrapper](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=hazard-crew&unit=scrapper&scene=counterplay&v=1): shoot the held crate to break its grip.
- [Harpooner](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=hazard-crew&unit=harpooner&scene=counterplay&v=1): let its harpoon latch, then shoot the yellow anchor to break the cable.
- [Sapper](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/recoil-foundry/?test=hazard-crew&unit=sapper&scene=counterplay&v=1): shoot an attached charge to detach it; its fuse continues running.

Optional `gun=pistol`, `gun=shotgun`, `gun=nailgun`, `gun=twinbore`, `gun=carbine` or `gun=repeater` changes the private tool. Unknown or duplicate parameters, mixed modes and unsupported link versions fail closed. Pause and Reduced Effects are supported.
