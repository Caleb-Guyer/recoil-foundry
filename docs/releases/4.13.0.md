# Support Systems — 4.13.0

Four new upgrades offer control, target switching, stored damage and temporary protection. They enter fresh Campaign and Daily reward pools from zone two, after the first boss. Each has a fitting on the actual weapon or player, an upgrade comparison, a live firing-range demonstration and a Logbook document.

- **Collimator:** Hold a steady aim for 0.6 seconds to tighten the complete pattern by up to 50%. Sweeping your aim opens it again. Pellets, firing lanes, crossed trajectories, forward beams and rear beams keep their count and damage budget.
- **Heat Relay:** Requires Thermal Runaway. Defeat the tracked enemy with your beam to store half its heat. Tracking another exposed enemy within one second transfers it once. Charged lances can inherit heat when their next charge starts; shields and outer-ray kills cannot multiply the transfer.
- **Overkill Bank:** Store half the actual excess damage from a direct killing hit, after enemy armor. Spend the reserve on the next discharge for at most 50% bonus damage. Pellets, beam rays, rear fire and Backblast share the reserve. A boosted discharge cannot refill its own bank; fragments, echoes, reflected shots, returned orbit payloads and environmental kills cannot bank damage.
- **Scrap Armor:** Directly destroy a crate, cover, cracked panel or breakable terrain to collect one plate lasting four seconds. It absorbs one ordinary small enemy bullet, up to 20 damage. Heavy ammunition, shells, blades and environmental damage remain dangerous. Plates cannot stack or refresh; the collector recharges six seconds after pickup.

The mint strip on the weapon shows Collimator focus. Gold and orange fittings show stored overkill damage and relay heat. Mint plates beside the player show Scrap Armor. These indicators also work with reduced effects. Reserves pause with simulation time and reset on room transitions, Retry, Reforge, death and the menu.

Fresh Campaign seeds carry reward revision **88**. Existing Campaign seeds, Continue saves and replays retain their reward pool and sequence. All four fittings are available in legal Workshop builds. New Dailies use ruleset **88**; archived Daily identities retain their previous rewards and beam rules. Existing saves require no new fields or migration.
