# Recoil Trials — 4.6.0

Fresh Campaigns offer one optional movement course through an existing upper challenge door in room 7 or 19. The seed selects Launch shaft, Cargo crossing or Airborne targets, avoiding a scheduled maintenance shaft. The ground exit skips it. The first five rooms never contain a Recoil Trial. Older Continue saves and Recent-run replays retain their previous room rules; Daily, boss Practice, Workshop and Overtime keep their encounters.

- **Launch shaft:** climb six marked landings through warned presses. Each landing lights up in order. Safe outer pockets give space to wait for the machinery.
- **Cargo crossing:** land on five moving loads and reach the far dock. The lower floor returns you safely to the starting dock, resets the landing sequence and makes the attempt ineligible for mastery. Time continues.
- **Airborne targets:** break three physical targets during one flight, then land at the exit. Shots fired while grounded cannot damage them. Landing before all three break resets them and invalidates mastery. Targets do not attack or award enemy kills, healing, discoveries or combat commendations.

Completing a Campaign trial awards one bonus upgrade without healing, then rejoins the next main room. Taking the normal exit leaves the course unopened. **Pause → Leave trial** abandons an entered course without the bonus, preserving health and the gun. Each run still has twenty main rooms.

Clean clears within 35 seconds in Launch shaft, 30 seconds in Cargo crossing and 12 seconds in Airborne targets earn **Aeronaut** and **Transit** gun finishes and the **Skyline** outfit. These appear in Appearance and the existing end-of-run reward cards. No new gun stats or weapon unlocks are attached to mastery.

Clearing a course in Campaign opens it in **Practice → Recoil Trials**. Practice uses a starting gun you have unlocked, with full health and no upgrades, while keeping Continue available. Legitimate Practice completion can earn mastery. Campaign records compare identical starting guns and upgrade sets; Practice records compare the same starting gun. Faster clears replace the corresponding record, and equal times prefer fewer shots. At most 100 build records are retained. The Logbook includes actual rendered course photographs and recorded gun/build times.

Time and moving machinery freeze during pause and hitstop. Continue restarts the course layout while retaining accepted damage, time and shots from the saved attempt. Pending bonus rewards resume at the completed exit without repaying completion. Trial records and mastery survive progress export, restore and undo; older backups migrate to an empty trial profile. Preview links cannot write progress or rewards.

Isolated playtests use ordinary controls and the real renderer: `?test=recoil-trial&course=launch`, `?test=recoil-trial&course=cargo` and `?test=recoil-trial&course=airborne`. Add `&gun=shotgun` or `&gun=nailgun` to inspect another starting gun; preview access does not unlock it.
