# Training Drills — 4.16.0

Revealed support masteries in the Logbook now offer **Try this challenge**. Each optional drill provides its build and a small authored firing range:

- **Heat Relay:** burn one of three targets down, then switch within one second. Complete six actual heat transfers.
- **Overkill Bank:** finish the weak target to store excess damage, then spend it on the tougher target. Use eight actual charges.
- **Scrap Armor:** break cover for a plate and intercept timed enemy shots. Block five actual bullets. The regular four-second plate duration and six-second recharge apply.

Progress, health and a short cue sit below the range. Begin, pause and retry controls are local to the drill. Aim and fire with the mouse, use your configured keyboard bindings, use the controller sticks and trigger, or hold and drag on the range with touch controls for movement and jumping. Touch aiming gently snaps near a target mount so small screens remain usable. Reduced motion suppresses effects while keeping training playable. Loss of focus stops simulation and clears held inputs; disconnecting an active controller pauses the drill.

Each drill owns a separate Game and physics world. The Campaign stays paused, including its exact position, enemies, upgrades and counters. Back returns to the selected Logbook entry. Retrying resets only the drill, and leaving destroys its physics world and input listeners. Real weapon, cover, bullet collision and support mechanics feed the same counters used by masteries; training cannot save a checkpoint, unlock a cosmetic, discover an upgrade or alter your run's progress.

Undiscovered masteries conceal the drill action. Revealed and earned masteries both allow training. Direct play links expose their selected challenge locally as a marked sample record when returning to the Logbook; they do not add it to your collection. Existing mastery reward requirements are unchanged: earn appearances in Campaign or Daily.
