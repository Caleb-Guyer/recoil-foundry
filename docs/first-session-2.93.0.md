# First-session clarity — 2.93.0

19 September 2026. Implementation and an agent-run usability audit for release item 2. The user asked the agent to perform the audit when first-time-player feedback was unavailable. **These are five test scenarios, not five human participants.** The external observation requirement remains open under section 7 of the [release checklist](browser-release-checklist.md).

## Changes

- Added a Controls screen on the title, in Pause, and through **H**. A small recoil diagram explains the relationship between firing down and moving up. Base controls describe only movement, firing, room completion and upgrades; special weapon inputs appear only when equipped. Back returns to the screen that opened Controls. Pause and Controls focus Resume/Back instead of a sound checkbox.
- Added **Learn to play** for new profiles, plus **Controls → Try controls** for everyone on the title. The warm-up uses existing harmless Workshop targets and the starting gun. It checks actual movement, a jump, an emitted shot, airborne downward firing and a destroyed target. R and Reset warm-up restart the lessons; Done returns to the menu. It does not change the campaign checkpoint or saved Workshop build, or grant discoveries, victories, records or commendations.
- New normal runs can show one short contextual hint at a time in room one. Each hint expires after seven simulation seconds; players can dismiss guidance. Later rooms, Daily, Practice and test presets do not receive these opening hints. The first upgrade screen briefly explains that one selection stays on the gun for the run. Completing the opening or warm-up remembers the guidance state locally.
- Ready nearby cases and shootable electrical controls share small target brackets. Terminals use an upward arrow only within their real jump-interaction range. Shoot cues respect physical cover and do not reveal unready controls or future objectives. Existing warning sounds and activation responses remain intact; no permanent objective panel was added.
- Sound and Music show **On**, **Off**, or **Muted**. If the master Sound toggle is off while Music remains checked, Music correctly reads Muted and a short explanation identifies the Sound toggle. The title's Settings link also indicates a master mute. Existing preferences remain in force.

## Exits, interactions and scope

Normal room doors retain a lit right-arrow when clear. Challenge doors retain a hazard triangle, striped frame and bonus symbol; route doors use their existing distinct markings. Extraction and New Game+ retain separate labeled lifts, with continuing above and finishing to the right. The controls screen does not preview their future encounters. Existing detour/route/extraction/Overtime progression and physical-traversal tests remain part of the full suite.

New interaction tests cover unavailable versus ready controls, proximity, blocking cover, sealed cases and the terminal's exact 65-unit interaction distance. Existing event tests cover Blackout's hidden exit, relay activation, terminal reinforcements and feedback. No combat stats, first-room enemy AI, Daily reward sequence or campaign length changed; Daily remains ruleset 78.

## Validation and remaining acceptance

All **1,513 automated tests passed**, including guidance, Workshop isolation and controller-menu checks. The production build passed. The extraction test room was also visually checked: the upper New Game+ lift and lower Exit lift retain distinct, readable labels. Browser checks use Codex's Chromium-based in-app browser; no claim is made here about separate Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari installations or physical controller/touch devices.

The [five-player observation sheet](first-session-playtest.md) is deliberately unfilled. Real players still need to demonstrate that they understand the opening without coaching. Agent inspection and passing simulations cannot establish that human outcome.
